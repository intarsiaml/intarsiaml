# think2: thinking-on quality, intarsiaml vs the production vLLM (35B, spark2)

Lane `think2`, branch `think2` from origin/main `5d78422ae`. Measurement and docs only: no default flipped, no engine change. It closes the open cutover item "thinking-on quality was not measured" of `docs/results/2026-11-20-ab35e-cutover-scoreboard.md`.

## Summary

**With thinking on, intarsiaml (shipping config) and the production vLLM score the same on both sets, and no difference is beyond the noise of vLLM against itself.**

| set | A1 vLLM | A2 vLLM (same requests) | B intarsiaml | B vs A1 | B vs A2 | A1 vs A2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSM8K n=200, pinned, flexible score | 0.985 (197) | 0.985 (197) | 0.985 (197) | +0.000 (+-0.024) noise | +0.000 (+-0.024) noise | +0.000 (+-0.024) noise |
| BBH subset n=150 (5 reasoning tasks x 30) | 0.933 (140) | 0.920 (138) | 0.913 (137) | -0.020 (+-0.061) noise | -0.007 (+-0.064) noise | -0.013 (+-0.060) noise |

Binomial 2 SE of each score: GSM8K 0.017, BBH 0.041 / 0.044 / 0.046. The bracketed difference is localeval compare's 2 SE of the difference, and every verdict on the primary score is "noise" (`raw/compare-*.txt`). Both sets are close to the ceiling for this model with thinking on, so the test can only exclude a gap of about 4 to 6 points on BBH and about 2 to 3 points on GSM8K; it cannot see smaller ones.

- **Truncation** (`max_tokens` 16384 on both, `finish_reason: length`): GSM8K 0 / 0 / 0 (A1 / A2 / B); BBH 1 / 1 / 2 questions whose recorded (last) reply was cut, 4 in all on two distinct items, both `dyck_languages`. One item (`bbh-00028`) hits the cap on all three runs; B's second (`bbh-00013`) is an item vLLM finished in 14 103 and 14 082 tokens, 2 300 under the cap. Truncated replies score 0.
- **Length** (checkpoint tokenizer on `reasoning_content` and on the reply; the sum is within 3 to 4 tokens of the server's `completion_tokens` on average): mean thinking / answer tokens, GSM8K A1 1950 / 22.5, A2 2178 / 28.0, B 2094 / 33.9; BBH A1 1963 / 6.8, A2 2031 / 6.8, B 1978 / 6.8. B against A1 is +7.9 % on GSM8K (bootstrap 95 % 1.003 to 1.161), but A2 against A1 is **+11.9 %** (1.041 to 1.207, sign test p 0.019) and B against A2 is -3.5 %. The length gap between engines (think-len's +15 %) is no larger than vLLM's own run-to-run spread. On BBH the ratios are 1.008 (B / A1), 0.974 (B / A2) and 1.035 (A2 / A1).
- **Wall time** (incidental: one request at a time through localeval, the box quiet by fleetq, vLLM saw no foreign request in any window): GSM8K A1 4965 s, A2 5557 s, B 2934 s (B 0.56 of the A mean); BBH A1 4180 s, A2 4307 s, B 2248 s (0.53). Output tokens over wall time (incidental, includes prompt processing): GSM8K B 145 tok/s against A1 80 and A2 80, BBH B 133 against A1 71 and A2 71, about 1.8 x, in line with ab35e's one-stream cells.
- **Systematic differences**: (1) B writes a worked final answer more often (GSM8K 57 / 48 / 35 of 200 for B / A2 / A1; the worked answer is right 56 of 57, 47 of 48, 34 of 35), the thinking-off style effect of style7 and think-len, which the flexible score does not see and the old exact-match score (`gsm8k_bare_number`: 0.700 B, 0.815 A1, 0.745 A2) does (A1 vs B "worse", -0.115 +-0.085; A2 vs B and A1 vs A2 noise). (2) No early stops, no loops beyond the dyck cases above, no inline `<think>` in any reply, `reasoning_content` present on every reply of every run. (3) One format failure, on vLLM: `bbh-00002` in A2 answered `{"answer": ""}` after 11 111 thinking tokens (`finish_reason: stop`); B and A1 answered it right.
- **Disagreements** are few and mostly on `dyck_languages` and `geometric_shapes`: B against A1 7 of 150 BBH items and 2 of 200 GSM8K, A1 against A2 4 of 150 and 0 of 200; five examples below.

**Cutover reading**: for thinking-on quality there is no sign of a regression and nothing in favour of either engine; the remaining open items in ab35e's verdict (operation evidence, memory, real traffic) are not touched by this.

## Method

- **Servers**: A is spark2's production vLLM (`missioncntrl-guidance`, role `retro`, `0.26.1rc1.dev602`, recipe in `raw/recipes/A.txt`), only sent requests, never stopped or reconfigured. B is origin/main `5d78422ae` built on spark2 (fleetq #10486, stamp `think2-5d78422ae3f5`, `--load cpu --mem 55`, `MAX_JOBS=4`, 6 min with vLLM up, lowest MemAvailable 70 GB), shipping config `intarsia serve --backend cuda --model <HF snapshot 1355db6a of nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4> --port 8191 --host 0.0.0.0 --served-model-name guidance --max-seq 131072 --mem-budget 40 --profile-every 0 --max-batch 2 --prefix-store on`, no environment variables, current defaults (bf16 KV, MTP k=2, KV once, prefix store prefault). One fresh B server per window; A and B never ran at the same time. Same client (this Mac), same network path (`spark-fa00:8000` and `:8191`).
- **Harness**: localeval 0.4.0 `quality` (`/opt/code/deepeval`), `--thinking on --structured prompt --max-tokens 16384 --wait-idle 600 --recipe <A or B command line>`, each run in its own fleetq slot (`--host spark2 --quiet gpu,cpu`; A runs `--mem 2`, B runs `--mem 50`). `scripts/think2/window.sh` is `scripts/ab35e/window.sh` cut down to one localeval run per window, with the clock sampler and memory watchdog (kill below 15 GB) kept for B.
- **Sets**: GSM8K, the same 200 ids and the pinned prompt as ab35e (`--bench gsm8k --limit 200 --pin-gsm8k`, template sha `dd31b48d...`), scored `gsm8k_flexible`. The harder set is **BBH**: localeval has no MATH-500 or GPQA, and BBH is the reasoning set it has. Five tasks chosen before any run for multi-step reasoning, not for being easy: `dyck_languages`, `multistep_arithmetic_two`, `logical_deduction_seven_objects`, `geometric_shapes`, `tracking_shuffled_objects_seven_objects`, first 30 items of each (`--bench bbh --tasks ... --limit 30`, deepeval's 3-shot CoT prompt, answer as JSON). 150 items.
- **Sampling**: **greedy**, the same as ab35e and think-len. localeval `quality` sends `temperature: 0` and nothing else (no top_p, top_k, seed or penalty; `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking: true`), and there is no flag for sampling, so the checkpoint's recommended thinking sampling (`generation_config.json`: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, applied by both servers only to requests that carry no values) was not exercised, and the harness was not patched. B's output is deterministic run to run (think-len), vLLM's is not, which is why A ran twice.
- **Order**: BBH A1, B, A2, then GSM8K A1, B, A2, each its own fleetq job (lane `drill` also used spark2 and fleetq ordered us; the queue wait is not in the wall times). vLLM's own request counters at the start and end of each window (`raw/window-*.log`) grew by our requests only (200 in a GSM8K A window, 152 in a BBH A window, 0 in a B window; the 2 extra BBH calls are explained next), so no other tenant sent it traffic during our windows.
- **Calls against questions (BBH)**: each BBH run made 152 calls for 150 questions (`speed.calls` of every BBH `result.json`; B's own counters: 152 requests, 148 `stop` and 4 `length`). The recorded truncations of B are 2, so its 4 length-cut calls are 2 questions asked twice: deepeval asks again when a reply does not parse as the schema answer, and at temperature 0 the retry is cut at the same place. The "truncated" figures in this document count questions (the recorded last reply); the 2 extra calls per BBH run cost the same tokens again in the wall time. For A the same 2 extra calls were made (vLLM's counter moved by 152), which fits one truncated question retried plus one other retry; vLLM's finish reasons were not snapshotted, so that is not established. GSM8K made 200 calls on every run.
- **Smoke first** (`raw/smoke-*`, `--no-unim`, 3 GSM8K and 5 BBH items on both engines): `reasoning_content` non-empty and `<think>` absent from the reply on both, `finish_reason: stop`, answers extracted and scored.
- **Analysis**: `scripts/think2/analyze.py` (run in the localeval venv with the checkpoint's `tokenizer.json`; scores, binomial 2 SE, truncation, thinking and answer tokens, McNemar and sign tests, bootstrap ratio of mean lengths, disagreements) and `scripts/think2/compare.sh` (localeval compare on the three pairs). The score of a GSM8K reply is `flexible`; a reply cut at the cap scores 0 (localeval 0.4.0).
- **Box**: lowest MemAvailable in a B window 38.7 GB (kill line 15 GB, 0 kills, 0 warn or error lines in either serve log); SM clock min 2379 MHz (BBH) and 2392 MHz (GSM8K) at utilisation above 90 %, mean 2392.

## Caveats

- **Greedy only** (harness limit, above). Thinking mode with the checkpoint's sampling (T 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20) may behave differently, in particular for loops at the cap; it was not run.
- **Near the ceiling**: GSM8K 0.985 on all three runs, BBH 0.91 to 0.93; the 2 SE of a BBH score is 4 to 5 points, of a difference 6 points. This shows "no large gap", not "equal". The only errors left are three GSM8K questions and the dyck and geometric items. A harder set (MATH-500, GPQA) would need to be added to localeval.
- **BBH subset**: 5 tasks x 30 items, first items of each task, chosen by hand for reasoning load; it is not the full BBH average and not comparable with published BBH numbers (deepeval's 3-shot prompt, JSON answer, greedy, 16 384 cap).
- **vLLM noise is measured once** (two runs on the same requests); a longer null would tighten the length comparison (think-len's open item (a)). The +7.9 % against A1 and -3.5 % against A2 on GSM8K say the engines are inside that spread, not that B is shorter.
- **Single stream**: localeval runs one request at a time; B's `--max-batch 2` second slot and the store under thinking-length conversations were not exercised. Wall time is incidental.
- **Cap**: 16 384 tokens removed truncation on GSM8K entirely but not on dyck, where a few traces loop or re-count past it on either engine.
- **No decision**: nothing was flipped; the row in `docs/decisions/LOG.md` records the measurement.
