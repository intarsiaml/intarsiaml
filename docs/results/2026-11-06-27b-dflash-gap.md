(`mtp pipe step`) is 68.2 ms a pass at k2, as the eager 7.7 + 60.3.

The bf16 head's row kernel is bandwidth-bound (flat to M=6); the MXFP4 GEMV's multi-row form (`mxfp4_gemv_impl<8>`, one simdgroup per output row, every
activation row's bf16 loaded and widened per output row) is ALU/issue-bound: its cost is nearly linear in M. That is the per-row slope above (MXFP4
matmuls are ~57 % of a plain step's op table, `raw/metal-vcurve/optable-k0.err`).

Metal decomposition at natural-v1 4k: 90.3 / 31.1 = 2.9x = acceptance (TF ~4.2 tokens a round / our 2.1-2.4 a pass: ~1.9x) x cost (our pass 2.0x a
plain step / TF's round 1.35x: ~1.5x). Base step: parity. We have no DFlash2 on Metal (load gate: cpu and HIP; no Metal `DynConv`).
