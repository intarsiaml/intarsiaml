Observed (static disassembly, whole kernel bodies):
- Activations are E4M3 fp8 with an f32 scale per row or per (row, 128 K) (W4A8, not Quark's W4A4); weights OCP MXFP4, pre-shuffled.
- Inner products: v_wmma_f32_16x16x16_fp8_fp8 or v_dot4_f32_fp8_fp8 only; no iu8/iu4/bf16 WMMA, no v_dot2/v_dot8.
- FP4 decode: 3-bit magnitude -> v_perm_b32 from an 8-byte table pre-shifted by the e8m0 scale (scale folded into the fp8 weight), sign via
  v_and_or 0x80808080, two more v_perm to reorder; per-row factor applied once at the end.
- gemv_kernel<{1,2,4,8},{1,2,4},bool> (doc string: "OCP-MXFP4 GEMM for ONE TO EIGHT rows: the weight streamed straight to registers and
  folded to e4m3 there, no LDS staging; WMMA when K is a multiple of 128, v_dot4 otherwise, chosen from K alone so a row's bits do not
  depend on M"). <8,1>: 320 v_dot4, 80 v_perm, 73 VGPR, 4 KB LDS. gemvw_kernel: the WMMA form (8 chained WMMAs per 128 K).
- decode_kernel<{64,128},{1..16 split-K},{1..8 row tiles},..>: split-K across workgroups, LDS-staged fp8 weights, WMMA.
- Which kernel the engine dispatches for its 8-row verify step was not determined (host code not disassembled).
