## Headline

- **The 27B loads and runs at tp=2.** It needed two core changes, both opt-in through `--tp 2` (section 6):
  - shard-aware MXFP4 loading;
  - an MXFP4 `down_proj` into the f32 partial.
  - No kernel changed.
- **tp=2 output is bitwise identical to tp=1 on every check run:**
  - 256 greedy ids on the short and 4k prompts;
  - the accuracy dumps on the golden's 10 prompts (60 tensors);
  - the KL numbers to every printed digit.
  - This holds with and without graphs and fusion.
  - Why (section 3): under W4A4 every MXFP4 product is exact, and so are its f32 sums. The row-parallel split therefore
    does not change a single rounding.
  - A discriminating run (window 6) confirms it. With 16-bit activations (W4A16, opt-in), tp=2 is *not* bitwise tp=1:
    - the short ids diverge at token 222;
    - self-golden mean KL21 is 1.3e-3 with 5 flips;
    - both tp=1 and tp=2 still pass the W4A16 golden gate.
- **Speed, tp=2 against tp=1.** Graphs + `INTARSIA_TP_FUSION=1`:
  - decode +42 % to +44 % (46.3 / 45.6 / 43.9 tok/s at 1k / 4k / 16k);
  - prefill +43 % to +44 %.
  - Graphs alone: decode +31 % to +33 %.
  - Every arm passed `--require-graphs`.
- **radiance is still about 3x faster** (144-157 tok/s with 7 MTP tokens).
  - Section 4 shows where a tp=2 step goes.

## 3. Why tp=2 is bitwise tp=1 on this model

At tp=2 a row-parallel projection (attention o_proj, GDN out_proj, FFN down_proj) works in four steps:
1. Each rank computes its half of the dot product over K into an f32 partial.
2. The P2P kernel adds the two partials in f32.
3. The sum is rounded once to bf16.
4. At tp=1 the same projection is one f32 accumulation, rounded once.

Under W4A4 the operands are exact on a coarse grid (window 6 below tests this explanation against W4A16):
- every weight is an e2m1 value (±{0, 0.5, ..., 6}) times a power of two;
- every activation is QDQ'd to the same kind of value per 32-group. The column split keeps whole groups, so each rank's
  QDQ is the full QDQ's slice; this is checked bitwise in `quant_mxfp4_tp.rs`.

So every product is a 2-bit mantissa times a power of two. Their sums stay exact in f32 as long as the exponent range a
row spans fits the 24-bit mantissa, which the identical dumps say it does here. Then the order of the sum, and the split
into two partials, does not change any bit.

The other ops are row- or head-local. The lm_head is unsharded on rank 0.

Limits of this argument:
- It is evidence from these prompts, not a proof for all inputs. A row whose 32-group scales span more than about 2^20
  would round.
- With W4A16 (`--mxfp4-act none`) the activations are not on the grid. tp=2 is then tp=1 only up to the f32 sum order.
  This is measured on the GPU (window 6):
  - the short-prompt ids diverge at token 222;
  - the self-golden residual is 1.3e-3 mean KL21, against 2.6e-12 under W4A4.
- The CPU model test agrees: W4A16 gives rel l2 7.9e-7; W4A4 gives exactly 0.

- **The head imbalance.**
  - Rank 1's step is 4.4 ms shorter in kernels: it holds no lm_head.
  - It then waits 8.0 ms per step, mostly at the first all-reduce of the next step, for rank 0's 4.0 ms bf16 head and
    argmax.
  - A vocab-parallel lm_head (each rank 124k vocab rows, then an argmax / logit gather) would save about 2 ms per step,
    roughly +9 %. The logits keep their bits.
- **The byte floor.**
  - Per rank per token: 6.47 GB of MXFP4 text layers, plus 2.54 GB of head on rank 0.
  - At 627 GB/s that is 14.4 ms, about 69 tok/s.
  - Bench rates, on the same basis for both arms: tp2gf at 1k is 67 % of that floor (46.3 / 69.4); tp1 is 80 % of its own
    (32.5 / 40.5).
  - The halved shapes stream less well:
    - o_proj / out_proj into the f32 partial: K=3072, about 400 GB/s;
    - down_proj: K=8704 is just over the LDS kernel's K <= 8192 cap, so it takes P1's pre-pass + plain GEMV path, at
      about 550 GB/s.
- **All-reduce.** 128 per step (2 per layer), median about 6 us each, 1.4-1.7 ms per step.

## 8. Plan: MTP / speculative decode for this model (stretch: written up, not built)

What exists, and what blocks this model:

- Today's speculation is G1 (`crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs`): n-gram prompt lookup, greedy exact-match acceptance, a
  verify pass of 1 + k rows (`Model::forward_verify`). It is bitwise the plain greedy stream because every verify row runs
  a row-exact kernel (`OpCall::exact_rows`, `KernelImpl::rows_exact`).
- Three gates stop it on this model:
  - `load.rs` allows `--spec` only on Metal at tp=1. No HIP kernel is registered `rows_exact`.
  - `engine.rs` refuses any model with recurrent state. Nothing rolls back the GatedDeltaNet state after a rejected
    draft: the 4 recurrent snapshots serve prefix reuse.
  - `Model::enable_verify` is single-rank.
- The checkpoint ships one MTP layer (`mtp_num_hidden_layers: 1`, `mtp_use_dedicated_embeddings: false`). All 15
  `mtp.*` tensors are bf16 and excluded from quantisation:
  - `fc` [5120, 10240];
  - the embedding / hidden pre-norms;
  - one gated full-attention layer (q 12288, k / v 1024, o 6144 input);
  - a dense MLP (17408).
  - In total about 425 M parameters, 0.85 GB bf16.
- The head shares the main model's embedding and lm_head. The loader skips it today.
- radiance (stilldeadcode/radiance 1.9.0 on tatooine) runs `--num-speculative-tokens 7` with its own draft pass:
  - its startup log shows a windowed `kv_draft` cache and a recorded "draft" graph, with no draft model given, so the
    draft is almost certainly this MTP head;
  - it also uses fp8 KV and a lossy 6-bit rotated all-reduce (`--tp-wire`);
  - it samples (temp 0.7) and logs no acceptance rate.

**The cost model at tp=2.** All of these numbers are estimates from this lane's measurements, not measurements of
speculation:

- **Main model step.** With graphs + fusion, the step is the tp=2 decode step in section 1 (23.2 ms after a 1k prompt, profile in section 4). Rank 0 carries the whole
  2.54 GB bf16 lm_head: 4.0 ms per token, with rank 1 idle (section 4).
- **One draft token.**
  - The MTP layer's own weights are 0.42 GB per rank when sharded like a main layer (fc column-split, attention and MLP
    as in `load_shard`): about 0.7 ms.
  - The draft also needs the lm_head. The bf16 head on rank 0 costs 4.0 ms per draft token, so 7 drafts would be about
    30 ms. That costs more than the main step, and **the draft head's cost is the first design problem**. Options:
    - (a) a vocab-parallel lm_head over both ranks (2.0 ms; it also speeds up plain decode, see section 5);
    - (b) a draft-only quantised copy of the head (MXFP4 0.66 GB: about 1 ms on one rank, 0.5 ms split). Verification
      keeps the bf16 head, so the output stays exact;
    - (c) a trimmed draft vocabulary (EAGLE-3 style top-32k ids).
    - (a) + (b) gives about 1.3 ms per draft token.
- **Verify pass of 1 + k rows.** The MXFP4 weights are read once for all rows; the HIP GEMV already takes M <= 8. The
  attention and GDN rows add work, and the delta rule is sequential over the rows. Estimate: 1.2 to 1.4x a 1-row step.
- **Throughput.** For k = 3 and a per-token acceptance a of 0.7 to 0.8, the expected tokens per pass are
  L = (1 - a^(k+1)) / (1 - a) = 2.5 to 3.0. Tokens per second are then about L / (1.3 * step + k * 1.3 ms):
  - from a 21.6 ms tp=2 step (window-1 short-prompt rate with graphs + fusion, 46.3 tok/s): 78 to 93 tok/s;
  - with k = 7 and a = 0.8: about 100 tok/s.
- **Against radiance.** radiance's 144-157 tok/s also rests on a faster base step: fp8 KV, a lossy wire, and kernels of
  its own.

**Stages.** Each stage ships behind an opt-in flag and gates on bitwise greedy identity with speculation on and off,
as Metal G2 did. Numerics of every existing model stay as they are.

1. **S0, HIP row-exact verify kernels.** Register `rows_exact` multi-row variants (M = 2..8) whose rows are bitwise the
   1-row decode kernels' rows:
   - the MXFP4 GEMV: the M = 1 LDS kernel and the M > 1 plain GEMV share the per-row arithmetic (P1, section 3), which
     needs proving per output;
   - the pair op;
   - split-KV attention, the gated q prologue, rmsnorm / GLU;
   - the GDN conv + delta rule as a per-row loop.
   - Gate: admission at M = 1..8 against the 1-row kernel, bitwise.
2. **S1, recurrent-state rollback.** The verify pass writes the GDN state after every row: 24 v heads x 128 x 128 f32 =
   1.5 MB per layer per rank, 72 MB per row over the 48 GDN layers, plus the conv tails. Commit the state of the last
   accepted row by index; this follows vLLM's Mamba / GDN spec-decode layout. The CPU reference comes first.
   - Gate: draft, reject and commit sequences against plain decode, bitwise, on the tiny hybrid fixture.
   - The alternative is snapshot-and-replay, which costs a second partial pass on every rejection.
3. **S2, verify at tp=2.** The peers run the verify pass in lockstep. Its all-reduces are those of a (1 + k)-row decode,
   so it is graph-capturable per k, as H1 did for the 1-row step.
   - Gate: tp=2 spec-on vs tp=2 spec-off ids, bitwise.
4. **S3, n-gram first.** Turn on the existing G1 drafter for this model on HIP. It needs no new model code and measures
   S0-S2's verify cost and acceptance on input-grounded prompts (Metal: 1.06x open-ended, 2.5x grounded).
5. **S4, the MTP head.**
   - Loader: `mtp.*`, bf16, sharded like a main layer.
   - Draft forward: `h = fc([norm_emb(embed(x_next)); norm_hidden(h_last)])`, then one decoder layer with its own small
     KV cache, then the shared final norm and lm_head. It is chained k times.
   - CPU reference first, checked against HF transformers' MTP logits on a fixture.
   - Then the draft head (a) / (b) above.
   - Gates:
     - greedy identity with MTP drafting on vs off (exact by construction: the drafts only choose what is verified);
     - acceptance rate per k on localeval's prompt sets.
6. **S5, sampling.** Rejection sampling for temperature > 0, which is what radiance serves. Here the gate is
   distributional, not bitwise: KL of the emitted-token distribution against non-speculative sampling.

**Risks.**
- S1 is the large item: per-row state writes in the fused `gdn_conv_delta` kernel.
- The MTP head is trained against bf16 activations, so acceptance under W4A4 main-model activations is unknown and must
  be measured, not assumed.
- Spec at temperature 0.7 (radiance's setting) gives less than greedy.
