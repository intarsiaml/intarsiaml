# DeepSeek-V4.1 Flash TP=2 prefill against TensorFold (lane dsv41/prefill)

Date 2026-11-02. Head: see `git log dsv41/prefill`. Base: main 4794bbc05. Raw logs: `2026-11-02-dsv41-prefill/raw/` (nsys traces of rank 0 are gzipped CSVs, nothing over 4 MB).
Prompts: `2026-11-02-dsv41-prefill/prompt1k.txt` (1034 tokens with the chat prefix, 1024 raw) and `prompt4k.txt` (4106 / 4096), cut from this repo's own result docs by `raw/mkprompt.py` with the Mia tokenizer.

## Result in one paragraph

Prefill was slow because the routed-expert GEMM (gate/up) reads and decodes every expert's weights once per chunk, and our chunk is 512 rows against TensorFold's 2048: at 512 rows an expert sees about 12 rows, at 2048 about 48, so the same weight decode serves four times the rows. The GPU is 99 % busy in both engines (no host or launch problem, so graph-capturing prefill has nothing to win). Two levers landed:

1. **Default-on, bitwise unchanged:** the prompt MoE kernel keeps two k tiles of weight words in flight per warp (`PF` 1 -> 2) and the MoE launcher's prompt slice is 2048 rows (`kPromptRows`, was 512). Same window, chunk 512: 4.1K prefill 9.13 s -> 6.75 s (575 -> 729 tok/s by localeval), 1K 2.33 s -> 1.76 s (546 -> 679). Golden 640/640 identical to main's; 32K and 65K prompts bit-identical (all 16 top-5 logprobs) to main.
2. **Opt-in, changes long-prompt output:** `INTARSIA_DSV41_CHUNK=2048` (TensorFold's chunk). On top of lever 1: 4.1K 5.28 s (836 tok/s), 1K 1.51 s (759). Golden 640/640 is identical, but on 32K and 65K prompts the logprobs move (max 0.19 nats at 32K, greedy text diverges at the 7th token at 65K), and the same happens with main's binary, so it is the chunk size, not my kernel change. Stays opt-in; needs the MMLU gate and the user's call.

Against TensorFold (same prompts, same client): 1K 759 vs 1459 tok/s (was 545), 4K 836 vs 2074 (was 577). The gap went from 2.7x / 3.6x to 1.9x / 2.5x. About 60 % of the remaining 4K gap is the MoE gate/up kernel and the other MoE kernels (table below).

## What did not work

- **Larger MoE member groups (G = 4 member tiles, 4 column tiles, work groups of 64): slower.** Window 2 (job 7862), chunk 2048, 4K prefill 6.58 s (G = 2, NT = 8) vs 7.26 s. In window 3 every NT = 4 or NT = 2 variant was also slower (6.15-7.45 s vs 5.63-6.38 s); only deeper prefetch won. Likely register pressure: the 64-column tile halves the work per weight read while G = 4 doubles the accumulators. Reverted.
- **`INTARSIA_DSV41_F16W_ROWS` 32 or 64** (router gate kernel): 6.67 s and 6.65 s against 6.38 s at 16. The router GEMV is not rescued by more rows per read; it needs a tensor-core kernel (lever 4), not tried.
- **Chunk 2048 as the default**: dropped after the 32K/65K comparison above.
- **Graph or fused prefill**: not tried, nothing to win at 99 % busy.
- **First TensorFold window (job 7833)**: its 4K requests came back with 2048 cached tokens (the 4K prompt starts with the 1K prompt and TF reuses prefixes by 2048-token block), so it was discarded and redone with a unique first line (job 7840). The first nsys export also produced a 600 kB CSV that was a stale export; `nsys stats --force-export` fixed it.
