Limits: one seed, 5 prompts of 256 tokens, one visit per cell; the sampled outputs of different arms were not compared token for token, and the `reject` rule and temperature 1.0 were not run. Sampling is exact in law whatever the draft (lane ss), so a worse head can only cost acceptance, which did not move measurably.

**Recommendation Q3:** the quantised head does not measurably reduce sampled acceptance at T 0.7 (mean 68.0 / 67.5 % vs 68.2 %); that evidence supports making fp8 or mix the default for the 27B, with the caveat above. The default is the user's call; no flip made.
