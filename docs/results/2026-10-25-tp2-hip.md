# Tensor parallelism on both R9700s: the gate against tp=1, and what was built on top (lane tp2, 2026-10-25)

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B NVFP4 (and Qwen3-8B, Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4) on tatooine's two R9700s (gfx1201), `intarsia ... --backend hip --tp 2
--devices 0,1`: one process, two in-process ranks, HIP P2P all-reduce. Radiance was down for each window (`raw/downtime.log`).
Raw logs: `docs/results/2026-10-25-tp2-hip/raw/`.

## 0. What was already true on main

The brief said fusion at tp=2 is opt-in. It is not: `tp_fusion(env, "hip", tp)` has been on for HIP at `--tp 2` since lane HS
(`a7336525`, LOG 2026-10-21 19:50, user decision to soak and flip). `INTARSIA_TP_FUSION=0` opts out. So the "tp=2 with
fusion" arm is what main ships, and the "without fusion" arm (-5 to -12 % decode, `2026-10-21-h1-tp2-hip.md`) is the opt-out.
This lane's stage 1 therefore changes the rule to "HIP and `--tp` > 1" (the same behaviour: the HIP collective is two ranks) and
adds the gate the user chose on 2026-10-25.

## 1. The tp gate

### Decisions, in order

1. User, 2026-10-25 01:00 ("Gate vs tp1"): the gate is the KL change against our own tp=1 output within the noise envelope; fusion is
   default-on at tp>1.
2. Coordinator, after the first result (section 1.2): tp2-vs-tp1 KL cannot separate a 0.3 % partial-scale bug from reduction-order noise,
   so the gate is (1) mutants killed, including the 0.3 % partial-scale one, through a direct partial-sum check; (2) tp=2 run-to-run ids
   identical; (3) distance to vLLM no worse than tp=1; (4) KL(tp2 vs tp1) reported, not gating. Release blocker: the one 0x1016 crash.
3. Coordinator, after window w8 (LOG.md, supersedes the per-prompt part of 2.3): check 3 is the MEAN only, KL(tp vs vLLM) <= KL(tp1 vs
   vLLM) x 1.02, prefill and decode; the per-prompt ratios are report-only next to the vLLM re-run noise of the prompt. Why: the two
   tp=1 control variants meant to calibrate a per-prompt cap (`INTARSIA_NO_FUSION=1`, `--prefill-chunk 233`) were bitwise identical to the
   tp=1 reference (every KL, every row), so per-prompt noise is unmeasured, and a cap chosen after seeing the data would be tuned.

Mechanism in the repo: recipe key `[accuracy] tp_ref_noise_factor` + `refnoise::resolve_factor_tp` (report-only form of check 4, unset);
`scripts/tp2-hip/tp-gate.py` (check 3); `crates/intarsia-models/tests/tp_test_bug.rs` (CPU) and
`crates/intarsia-backend-hip/tests/tp_partial_sum.rs` (GPU) for check 1.

### 1.1 Check 3 (gating): mean distance to vLLM

35B NVFP4, short golden, `raw/w8/acc-ext-*.out`, `raw/w8/../gate-check3.txt` (`tp-gate.py --noise ...`). tp=2 runs are bitwise run to run:

| mode | tp=1 KL21 vs vLLM | tp=2 | ratio (limit 1.02) |
|---|---|---|---|
| prefill (1441 positions) | 5.978e-3 | 5.967e-3 | 0.998 PASS |
| decode (160 positions) | 12.14e-3 | 7.10e-3 | 0.585 PASS |

Per prompt, report only (prefill ratio, and (tp2 - tp1) in units of that prompt's vLLM re-run noise): most prompts are within about
+-1.4 noise units; `chat_short` -2.6 and `chat_multiturn` **+7.1** (tp=2 6.35e-2 vs tp=1 3.27e-2, noise 4.35e-3) are the outliers, `code`
is 1.60x but +1.1 units. Decode has 16 scored positions per prompt and no recorded per-prompt noise; its ratios run from 0.21 to 5.3.
The tp=1 controls are bitwise the reference (so they say nothing about per-prompt noise): this is a real gap in the evidence, and
`chat_multiturn` is the first prompt to look at when someone has a window to spend on it.

### 1.2 Check 3 does not detect a 0.3 % to 1 % partial-scale bug

Same command with `INTARSIA_TP_TEST_BUG=scale:F` (rank 1's partials before every all-reduce; eager, no graphs):

| bug | prefill mean ratio | decode mean ratio |
|---|---|---|
| none (tp=2) | 0.998 | 0.585 |
| 0.1 % | 1.059 (fails) | 0.504 |
| 0.3 % | 0.899 | 1.245 (fails) |
| 1 % | 0.822 | 0.988 |

The bugged runs have a lower prefill KL than clean tp=2, and the decode column is not monotonic in the size of the bug: noise, not
detection. The same is true of KL(tp2 vs tp1) (check 4: tp=2 5.261e-3 = 1.66x the vLLM re-run noise, CUDA tp=2 1.10x; bug 0.3 %
4.471e-3, 1 % 4.671e-3; `raw/w1/self-tp2-*`). Check 1 carries the bug.

### 1.3 Check 1 (direct, gating): the partial sums

- CPU, `tp_test_bug.rs`, f32 tiny model, tp=2 vs tp=1 logits, bound 6 x 2 x gamma_63 = 4.5e-5 (rel_l2): worst clean 5.8e-7 (78x under), weakest
  `scale:1.003` 1.08e-3 (24x over), weakest `scale:1.001` 3.6e-4 (8x over), 8 seeds.
- GPU, `tp_partial_sum.rs`, w8 (`raw/w8/hiptest-tp-partial-sum.log`): the registry-selected bf16 GEMV with f32 output at K = 2048 and 5120,
  m = 1, 8, 64, tp=2 = each rank's half of K then the real P2P `all_reduce_sum_into` (f32 to f32 and f32 to bf16), against the full-K GEMV
  of tp=1. Per element, deterministic bound `BOUND_DET = 2 gamma_K sum|x w|` (gamma_K = K u / (1 - K u), u = 2^-24) and probabilistic bound
  `BOUND_PROB = 2 gamma~_K(lambda=6) sum|x w|` (Higham & Mary 2019; assumes zero-mean independent rounding errors). Clean: max|y2 - y1| is at
  most 0.002 of BOUND_DET in every case (same-sign and random-sign data). Mutants (scale on rank 1's partial, drop a rank, swap x halves):
  - 0.3 %: same-sign 6.3x BOUND_DET at K=2048 and 2.5x at K=5120 (killed by the deterministic bound); random-sign 1.09x at K=2048 (killed),
    0.26x BOUND_DET but **3.1x BOUND_PROB** at K=5120 (killed by the probabilistic bound only: that is the gating bound at K=5120);
  - 0.1 %: same-sign 2.1x at K=2048, 0.83x BOUND_DET but 9.95x BOUND_PROB at K=5120;
  - drop a rank, swap halves: killed by 90x to 2000x or more on both kinds of data.
  Not detectable by any summation-order bound: a partial scale below about 4 gamma~ (6.5e-5 at K=2048, 1e-4 at K=5120). The probabilistic
  bound rests on an independence assumption, not a theorem.

### 1.5 The crash

One of the first tp=2 accuracy runs in w1 died: `HSA_STATUS_ERROR_EXCEPTION code 0x1016`, exit 139, immediately after the "this build:" line,
the first tp=2 process after a parallel tp=1 prelude (35B on GPU 0, 8B on GPU 1), default config. Window w8 repeated the exact command: cold start after
the same prelude 0/6, warm back to back 0/24, `INTARSIA_TP_VOCAB=0` 0/12, main (before this lane) 0/12: **0 of 54**. The cause is unexplained.
Recorded as known, unreproduced. (The same exception is what `drop-captured-allreduce` and the stale-position mutant produce on purpose; the
bounded wait in `allreduce.hip` traps after about a minute of spinning.)

## 3. Speed, Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4 (AWQ-Quark), tp=1 vs tp=2 (window w11, 10:03 to 10:36, `raw/speed-1003`)

Same harness as section 2: single stream, `--gen 512`, 3 interleaved rounds per arm, median (min-max in the logs). Arms: tp1; tp2 (defaults);
tp2-v0 (`INTARSIA_TP_VOCAB=0`, replicated lm_head); tp2-f0 (`INTARSIA_TP_FUSION=0`); tp2-a1 (`INTARSIA_TP_ARNORM=1`, fused all-reduce+rmsnorm).

| metric | tp1 | tp2 | tp2 / tp1 | tp2-v0 | tp2-f0 | tp2-a1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| prefill 1k tok/s | 978 | 1406 | 1.44 | 1405 | 1398 | 1404 |
| prefill 4k | 1017 | 1465 | 1.44 | 1460 | 1457 | 1463 |
| prefill 16k | 925 | 1316 | 1.42 | 1313 | 1308 | 1309 |
| decode @1k tok/s | 32.9 | 53.2 | 1.62 | 46.5 (-12.6 %) | 48.5 (-8.8 %) | 53.7 (+0.9 %) |
| decode @4k | 32.3 | 52.6 | 1.63 | 46.0 | 47.9 | 53.0 |
| decode @16k | 30.8 | 49.8 | 1.62 | 44.0 | 45.8 | 50.1 |

- tp=2 is 1.42x to 1.44x faster in prefill and 1.62x faster in decode; the vocab-parallel head is worth 13 % of decode on this model, fusion 9 %.
  The fused all-reduce+rmsnorm kernel is +0.9 % (round spread under 0.5 %): kept opt-in.
- **The gap to Radiance is large and is not tp**: Radiance 1.12.0 (2026-10-25, with DFlash) prefill is 5210 / 6133 / 5852 tok/s at 1k / 4k / 16k, so
  our tp=2 prefill is 3.7x / 4.2x / 4.4x lower. Its 1-stream decode of 145 tok/s includes speculation; a no-spec Radiance decode number was not measured here,
  so decode is not compared. 27B MXFP4 prefill at tp=1 (978 tok/s) is a single-card kernel problem (the MXFP4 matmul), outside this lane.
- **`--max-batch` on 27B MXFP4 is refused at load, at tp=1 and tp=2 alike**: the batch rows (b16 phase: 4 and 16 streams) never ran. Error:
  "a 9-row batched step runs matmul_mxfp4 on matmul_mxfp4_mma, which is not a row-exact kernel ... rows would not be bitwise the solo step"
  (`raw/speed-1003/serve-fail-tp1.log`, `serve-fail-tp2.log`). The bitwise-equals-solo rule holds, so batching 27B needs a row-exact MXFP4 rows kernel
  (the mr lane's "MXFP4 rows variant", job #5280); it is not a tp problem and the tp=2 batch step itself is already covered by the 35B tests.
  Radiance reference for batching: 383.9 / 580.1 tok/s aggregate at 4 / 16 streams (with DFlash).
