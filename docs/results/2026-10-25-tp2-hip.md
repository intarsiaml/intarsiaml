# Tensor parallelism on both R9700s: the gate against tp=1, and what was built on top (lane tp2, 2026-10-25)

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B NVFP4 (and Qwen3-8B, Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4) on tatooine's two R9700s (gfx1201), `intarsia ... --backend hip --tp 2
--devices 0,1`: one process, two in-process ranks, HIP P2P all-reduce. Radiance was down for each window (`raw/downtime.log`).
Raw logs: `docs/results/2026-10-25-tp2-hip/raw/`.

## 0. What was already true on main

The brief said fusion at tp=2 is opt-in. It is not: `tp_fusion(env, "hip", tp)` has been on for HIP at `--tp 2` since lane HS
(`a7336525`, LOG 2026-10-21 19:50, user decision to soak and flip). `INTARSIA_TP_FUSION=0` opts out. So the "tp=2 with
fusion" arm is what main ships, and the "without fusion" arm (-5 to -12 % decode, `2026-10-21-h1-tp2-hip.md`) is the opt-out.
This lane's stage 1 therefore changes the rule to "HIP and `--tp` > 1" (the same behaviour: the HIP collective is two ranks) and
adds the gate the user chose on 2026-10-25.

## 1. The tp gate

User decision, 2026-10-25, option "Gate vs tp1": the gate for `--tp` > 1 is the KL change against the engine's own tp=1 output,
within the reference noise envelope; plus 256 ids identical at tp=2 run to run, the prefix-reuse gate at tp=2, and the comm and
sharding mutants from G1b; tp=1 stays byte-identical to main.

Implemented as a mechanism (`intarsia accuracy --self-golden TP1_DUMP --self-vs-ref-noise` already existed; new here):

- recipe key `[accuracy] tp_ref_noise_factor` (`crates/intarsia-core/src/recipe.rs`, `docs/recipes.md`) and
  `refnoise::resolve_factor_tp`: for a `--tp` > 1 `--self-vs-ref-noise` run it replaces the golden's and the recipe's plain factor;
  `--ref-noise-factor` still wins; tp=1 never sees it (unit tests).
- `scripts/tp2-hip/stages-w1.sh` is the gate as a window stage: tp=1 dump, tp=2 dump against it, the same at factor 1.0, two deliberate
  partial-scale bugs (`INTARSIA_TP_TEST_BUG`), run to run, opt-outs.

### Result: the gate as built FAILS

HIP tp=2 against tp=1, short golden, the same build (`raw/w1/self-tp2-vs-tp1-short*.out`, verdict
`verdict-gate-short.json`); reference noise 3.161e-3 (vLLM re-run, 1441 positions):

| run | prefill mean KL21 | decode mean KL21 | prompt `code`, KL / (3 x its noise) prefill, decode | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tp=2 vs tp=1, factor 1.0 | 5.261e-3 = **1.66x** | 4.108e-3 = 1.30x | 1.505, 1.121 (limit 1) | **FAIL** (mean, and prompt `code`) |
| tp=2 vs tp=1, factor 2.0 (what-if) | PASS | PASS | 1.505, 1.121 | **FAIL** (prompt `code`) |
| tp=2 + `scale:1.003` on rank 1's partials vs tp=1 | 4.471e-3 | 4.854e-3 | 1.046, 1.313 | FAIL (prompt `code`) |
| tp=2 + `scale:1.01` vs tp=1 | 4.671e-3 | 8.013e-3 (FAIL at factor 2) | 1.391, 4.231 | FAIL |

Reading it:

1. This is the number H1 measured on 2026-10-21 (1.664x, `code` 1.51x); nothing got worse. CUDA tp=2 vs tp=1 was 1.10x
   (`2026-10-21-35b-tp2-hip.md`), and H1 found an equally correct tp=1 variant at 1.51x, so the box where tp=2 stands is
   "above CUDA, at the edge of what valid tp=1 variants do".
2. **tp=2 is about as far from vLLM as tp=1.** `accuracy` against the vLLM golden, short set, prefill 21-way KL: tp=1 5.978e-3, tp=2
   5.967e-3, decode 12.14e-3 and 7.10e-3, all far inside the 3e-2 floor (`raw/w1/acc-tp1-batch-35b-short.out`,
   `raw/vocab/acc-tp2-on-35b-short.out`). H1's all-rows KL21 against the same golden had tp=1 6.128e-3 and tp=2 6.917e-3 (+13 %,
   `2026-10-21-35b-tp2-hip.md`). Either way tp=1 and tp=2 are about as far from each other (5.3e-3) as each is from vLLM (6e-3):
   the difference is summation order amplified by the recurrent state and MoE routing (16 top-1 flips in 1441 positions, one
   position at KL 0.73), not a bias. The long set, where the noise is larger, passed in H1 (0.89x); section 6 repeats it.
3. **The KL gate does not separate tp=2 from a bug.** A 0.3 % scale error on every one of rank 1's all-reduce inputs passes the mean
   gate in both modes, and scores a *lower* prefill KL than the clean run; 1 % passes prefill. (The bug runs are eager and
   host-scaled; graphs are bitwise the eager path, `2026-10-21-h1-tp2-hip.md`.) The correctness evidence has to come from the
   exactness checks and the mutants (sections 2 and 6), not from this threshold.
4. The 2.0 that would let the mean pass is a number picked after seeing 1.66x. `tp_ref_noise_factor` is therefore left unset in
   `recipes/qwen3_5_moe.toml`, the gate fails, and the tolerance is an open decision (LOG.md, open decisions).
