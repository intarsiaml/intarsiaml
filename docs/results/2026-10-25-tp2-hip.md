Raw logs: `docs/results/2026-10-25-tp2-hip/raw/`.

## 0. What was already true on main

The brief said fusion at tp=2 is opt-in. It is not: `tp_fusion(env, "hip", tp)` has been on for HIP at `--tp 2` since lane HS
(`a7336525`, LOG 2026-10-21 19:50, user decision to soak and flip). `INTARSIA_TP_FUSION=0` opts out. So the "tp=2 with
fusion" arm is what main ships, and the "without fusion" arm (-5 to -12 % decode, `2026-10-21-h1-tp2-hip.md`) is the opt-out.
This lane's stage 1 therefore changes the rule to "HIP and `--tp` > 1" (the same behaviour: the HIP collective is two ranks) and
adds the gate the user chose on 2026-10-25.

## 1. The tp gate

### Decisions, in order

1. User, 2026-10-25 01:00 ("Gate vs tp1"): the gate is the KL change against our own tp=1 output within the noise envelope; fusion is
   default-on at tp>1.
2. Coordinator, after the first result (section 1.2): tp2-vs-tp1 KL cannot separate a 0.3 % partial-scale bug from reduction-order noise,
   so the gate is (1) mutants killed, including the 0.3 % partial-scale one, through a direct partial-sum check; (2) tp=2 run-to-run ids
   identical; (3) distance to vLLM no worse than tp=1; (4) KL(tp2 vs tp1) reported, not gating. Release blocker: the one 0x1016 crash.
3. Coordinator, after window w8 (LOG.md, supersedes the per-prompt part of 2.3): check 3 is the MEAN only, KL(tp vs vLLM) <= KL(tp1 vs
   vLLM) x 1.02, prefill and decode; the per-prompt ratios are report-only next to the vLLM re-run noise of the prompt. Why: the two
   tp=1 control variants meant to calibrate a per-prompt cap (`INTARSIA_NO_FUSION=1`, `--prefill-chunk 233`) were bitwise identical to the
   tp=1 reference (every KL, every row), so per-prompt noise is unmeasured, and a cap chosen after seeing the data would be tuned.

Mechanism in the repo: recipe key `[accuracy] tp_ref_noise_factor` + `refnoise::resolve_factor_tp` (report-only form of check 4, unset);
`scripts/tp2-hip/tp-gate.py` (check 3); `crates/intarsia-models/tests/tp_test_bug.rs` (CPU) and
`crates/intarsia-backend-hip/tests/tp_partial_sum.rs` (GPU) for check 1.

### 1.3 Check 1 (direct, gating): the partial sums

- CPU, `tp_test_bug.rs`, f32 tiny model, tp=2 vs tp=1 logits, bound 6 x 2 x gamma_63 = 4.5e-5 (rel_l2): worst clean 5.8e-7 (78x under), weakest
  `scale:1.003` 1.08e-3 (24x over), weakest `scale:1.001` 3.6e-4 (8x over), 8 seeds.
- GPU, `tp_partial_sum.rs`, w8 (`raw/w8/hiptest-tp-partial-sum.log`): the registry-selected bf16 GEMV with f32 output at K = 2048 and 5120,
  m = 1, 8, 64, tp=2 = each rank's half of K then the real P2P `all_reduce_sum_into` (f32 to f32 and f32 to bf16), against the full-K GEMV
  of tp=1. Per element, deterministic bound `BOUND_DET = 2 gamma_K sum|x w|` (gamma_K = K u / (1 - K u), u = 2^-24) and probabilistic bound
  `BOUND_PROB = 2 gamma~_K(lambda=6) sum|x w|` (Higham & Mary 2019; assumes zero-mean independent rounding errors). Clean: max|y2 - y1| is at
  most 0.002 of BOUND_DET in every case (same-sign and random-sign data). Mutants (scale on rank 1's partial, drop a rank, swap x halves):
  - 0.3 %: same-sign 6.3x BOUND_DET at K=2048 and 2.5x at K=5120 (killed by the deterministic bound); random-sign 1.09x at K=2048 (killed),
    0.26x BOUND_DET but **3.1x BOUND_PROB** at K=5120 (killed by the probabilistic bound only: that is the gating bound at K=5120);
  - 0.1 %: same-sign 2.1x at K=2048, 0.83x BOUND_DET but 9.95x BOUND_PROB at K=5120;
  - drop a rank, swap halves: killed by 90x to 2000x or more on both kinds of data.
  Not detectable by any summation-order bound: a partial scale below about 4 gamma~ (6.5e-5 at K=2048, 1e-4 at K=5120). The probabilistic
  bound rests on an independence assumption, not a theorem.

### 9.1 w19 (89c4ee1, `raw/w19`) and what `INTARSIA_HIP_POISON` does and does not cover

`INTARSIA_HIP_POISON=<hex byte>` fills every new `Backend::alloc` of the HIP backend (weights, workspace, kernel scratch the plan reserves, sequence states) at allocation time, not while a graph is
captured. It does **not** cover a region that is written once and reused: the workspace and scratch regions that successive ops and steps reuse keep the previous op's data, and nothing
refills them. So a NaN-filled run proves that no never-written memory is read; it says nothing about a read of stale data from an earlier op or step. The poison variants below therefore do not exclude
read-before-write, only its fresh-allocation form (the comm buffers are separate mallocs, zeroed explicitly).

| variant | result |
|---|---|
| size loop, allocations zero-filled (`00`), 12 reps | reps 0 and 1 ok; rep 2 hung and trapped 0x1016 about 60 s in, with no mismatch reported |
| size loop, allocations NaN-filled (`ff`), 3 reps | 0/3 (no NaN read, no mismatch) |
| solo only, 60 fresh states x 3 reps | 0/3: the first decode step of every fresh state is bit-identical to the first |
| solo plus one batched step of 3 rows between states | **FAILED at the first batched step of the process** (`batch 3 step 0 row 0 (sequence 0, context 10 -> 11)`, solo 1.229 batch 5.227, all rows wrong) |

The solo step is therefore not the problem (a wrong value that is consistent between states would still pass, but the accuracy gates cover that); the first batched step of fresh rows is. The minimal repro is
"fresh model, one solo state, one batched step of 3 fresh rows". w20 runs it with the per-rank collective ledger (`INTARSIA_TP_COLL_TRACE=1`, `Model::coll_trace_dump`: passes and cumulative collective
counts, eager launches plus graph replays) and a 25 s watchdog that dumps both ledgers; the first line where rank 0 and rank 1 differ names the step they decided differently.

**w19 e1 (the key result).** The size loop with every graph off (`Model::set_graphs(false)`, solo and batched, both ranks): **0/12 reps** failed; w18's `INTARSIA_BATCH_GRAPHS=0` (only the batched graphs off, the solo decode graphs
still on) failed at rep 0. So the mismatch needs the **solo decode graphs**: it appears when a batched step (eager or graph) runs after solo steps that replayed a captured graph, on a fresh batch of rows. Solo steps alone, with graphs,
are fine (180 states, bit-identical), and a batched step alone with every graph off is fine. `INTARSIA_SOLO_GRAPHS=0` (new, debugging) turns off only the solo graphs; w20 runs the 2x2.

## 10. The 27B tp=2 batched hang: cured by a process-wide graph lock (w26 to w31), and the final gates

**What was wrong.** On the 27B tp=2 reproduction (fresh solo states and batched steps in one model, the size loop) a captured solo decode graph intermittently replayed with kernels missing:
the collective launch counters one rank short by 10 or 61 launches for one row, then the ranks one launch apart, wrong logits in every row of the next batched step, and a collective that
waits until the bounded wait traps (`HSA_STATUS_ERROR_EXCEPTION 0x1016`). The captures themselves are consistent (the same 868/869 kernel nodes per solo graph on both ranks,
`INTARSIA_GRAPH_NODES=1`); the loss is in a replay.

**What did not help** (each ran at 20 reps against a control that hung within 1 to 8 reps): ending a peer step with a stream sync (`INTARSIA_TP_PEER_SYNC`), idling the stream before capture/first launch
(`INTARSIA_GRAPH_SYNC_CAPTURE`), idling before a graph exec is destroyed (`..._SYNC_DESTROY`), a 25 us sleep before the data reads (drain 5), warm-up, no deferred loading, leaking instead of freeing the comm,
host-pinned counters, `AMD_SERIALIZE_KERNEL=3`, `HIP_LAUNCH_BLOCKING=1` (w26 to w30 `raw/`), and a batch-level sync between steps (`lane-tp2/batch-sync`, hung at rep 15 of 40).

**What did.** (a) Solo decode graphs off at tp>1 (`INTARSIA_SOLO_GRAPHS=0` at any tp): 72 + 40 clean reps (w30 d1 to d3), costs about 3 % of 1-stream decode. (b) One HIP graph API call at a time
process-wide (`INTARSIA_GRAPH_LOCK`, `device.rs::graph_lock`, held across capture with instantiate and across each launch; default on, `=0` off), solo graphs on:

| w30/w31 run (27B tp=2, same binary) | graph lock | reps | failures |
|---|---|---|---|
| w30 k0 control, solo graphs on | off | hung | yes |
| w30 k5, k6 | on | 20 + 20 | 0 |
| w31 l1 (size loop) | on | 40 | 0 |
| w31 l2 (size loop) | on | 40 | 0 |
| w31 l3 (minimal repro: fresh model, one solo state, one batched step of 3 rows) | on | 40 | 0 |
| w31 l4 control, same window and binary | **off** | failed at rep 9 (batch 5 step 0, all 248320 logits wrong, then the watchdog and 0x1016) | yes |

160 clean reps with the lock against failures of every lock-off control within 1 to 9 reps. The mechanism is not proven: the evidence is that two rank threads capture and launch graphs concurrently and
that serialising those calls removes the failure, not a located runtime bug. With one rank (tp=1) the lock is never contended; tp=1 behaviour is byte-identical to main (below).

**Cost of the lock** (27B tp=2, 4k prompt, 512 generated tokens, two rounds each, `raw/speed-0225`; solo eager is the first fix, solo graphs plus lock the shipped one):

|---|---|---|---|---|

| solo graphs + lock (round 1 / 2) | 1466 / 1465 | 52.63 / 52.60 | 77.8 / 77.8 | 98.1 / 98.0 |
