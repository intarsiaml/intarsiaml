To determine the arrival time, we need to calculate the duration of each segment of the journey and add them to the departure time.

*   Distance: $84$ km
*   Speed: $60$ km/h
*   Time = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$
*   $t_1 = \frac{84}{60}$ hours

Simplify the fraction:
$\frac{84}{60} = \frac{7}{5} = 1.4$ hours

Convert $0.4$ hours to minutes:
$0.4 \times 60 = 24$ minutes

So, $t_1 = 1$ hour and $24$ minutes.

**Step 2: Account for the stop.**
*   Stop duration ($t_{stop}$) = $12$ minutes.

Convert $0.5$ hours to minutes:
$0.5 \times 60 = 30$ minutes

So, $t_2 = 30$ minutes.
