### Roofline

Bytes per pass: body 12.94 GB (E2M1 + e8m0 scales, `2026-10-27-cgv-cuda-mxfp4-gemv.md`) + fp8 head 1.27 GB = 14.21 GB for a verify pass or a plain step; the draft adds 2 x (0.85 GB bf16 MTP layer + 0.33 GB fp8 head rows) = 2.36 GB.
GB10 LPDDR5x peak is 273 GB/s; a plain coalesced read streams 239 GB/s at engine-like working sets (cgv, NB = 16 / 64), so 239 is the roofline used here.

| step | bytes | time at 239 GB/s | measured | share of roofline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| plain, 4k | 14.21 GB | 59.5 ms | 68.0 ms | 87 % |
| MTP k2 pass, 4k | 16.57 GB | 69.3 ms | 86.6 ms | 80 % |

By class (4k MTP k2): body GEMV 12.94 GB in 63.1 ms = 205 GB/s (86 % of 239; the plain step's M=1 kernel runs 218 GB/s, 91 %); lm_head 1.27 GB in 5.41 ms = 235 GB/s (98 %); draft head
0.67 GB in 2.77 ms = 242 GB/s (101 %); MTP layer bf16 1.7 GB in 7.0 ms = 243 GB/s (101 %). So the only classes under the roofline by more than a few percent are the body GEMV (9 ms a pass) and the non-bandwidth
items (attention 3.6, GDN 2.3, state copies 1.6, glue 1.2, idle 1.4 = 10 ms). The head, the draft head and the MTP layer already stream at the DRAM rate: the only way to make them cheaper is to move fewer bytes.

## 2. Candidates and pre-registered rules

Sized from section 1 before anything was measured.

| id | change | expected | numerics |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `INTARSIA_MTP_QUANT=fp8`: the MTP layer (fc, q/k/v/o, gate/up/down; 0.85 GB bf16) quantised to fp8 e4m3 at load, as the lm_head is | 1.7 GB -> 0.85 GB a pass: -3.5 ms of 86.6 (+4 %) minus any loss of acceptance | draft only; verified output is the main model's own rows, so ids are unchanged |
| B | block granularity of the MXFP4 GEMV: fewer warps per block, so the launch tail and ramp (one block's duration per launch, ~18 us x 496 launches = 9 ms a pass) shorten | up to -4 ms (+4 to 5 %) | none: per-warp code and reduction order unchanged, bitwise |
| C | CUDA `matmul_mxfp4_pair_gemv(_rows)` (the model already pairs gate/up, k/v, GDN qkv/z and b/a for MXFP4; the CUDA registry has no kernel, so it falls back to two launches): 176 fewer launches a pass | -2 to -3 ms (+2.5 to 3.5 %) | none, bitwise two GEMVs |
| E | `INTARSIA_VERIFY_ATTN_ROWS_CAPTURED=1`: the captured step's verify attention as one row-table launch per layer (rule below) | 16k: attention 16 ms of 101 per pass, K/V read 3x; 4k: 3.6 ms | none (bitwise the solo decode launch) |
| D | k3 instead of k2 (recipe): the multi-row GEMV and the fp8 head made extra rows cheap | measured above: +8.5 % (1k), +6.2 % (4k), one sample | none for ids; recipe default flip |

Rules (set before the A/B windows; ratios are paired medians of >= 3 ABAB rounds, `generate`, 4k and 1k, MTP k2, same window):

* **A** passes if decode >= +3.0 % at 4k and >= +2.0 % at 1k, acceptance falls by <= 2 points, ids == `--spec off` on short-0 and 4k-1 (256 ids). It is opt-in either way (a knob, default off). Below the bar it is reported as "no gain" and stays an unrecommended opt-in.
* **B** passes if decode >= +3.0 % with MTP k2 at 4k and plain decode does not regress, ids == `--spec off` on short-0 and 4k-1, `kernels test --quick` 0 FAIL. If it passes it is bitwise-neutral, so it may be a default.
* **C** passes if decode >= +2.0 % at 4k with MTP k2, ids as above, `kernels test --quick` 0 FAIL (new kernel: planted-mutant admission), the new kernel bitwise two single GEMVs in `tests/rows_registry`-style tests.
* **E** (added after the 16k profile, before any E measurement): `INTARSIA_VERIFY_ATTN_ROWS_CAPTURED=1`. Per-row verify attention is 3 launches pairs a layer in the captured step; 4k attention is 3.6 ms of 86.6 per pass, **16k attention is 16.0 ms of 100.9** (`raw/base16/breakdown-mtp-16k.txt`, 154 us a launch: the three rows re-read the same 1 GB of K/V). One row-table launch (bitwise the solo launch, already used by the eager verify pass) may share K/V between the rows through L2 and saves 64 launches a pass. Passes if decode >= +2.0 % at 4k and >= +4.0 % at 16k with MTP k2 (paired medians, >= 3 ABAB rounds), ids == `--spec off` on short-0 and 4k-1 plus a 16k id check, `kernels test --quick` 0 FAIL. It must show the captured engagement note in stderr (`engaged in the captured step`), otherwise the A/B measures nothing.
* **A2** (pre-registered before window `ab4`): `INTARSIA_MTP_QUANT=mxfp4` and `=mix` (fc and attention fp8, feed-forward mxfp4) are judged against `fp8` in the same window at 4k, MTP k2: a mode is worth listing only if it beats `fp8` by >= +1.0 % decode with acceptance down <= 2 points against base; otherwise `fp8` stays the only recommended mode and the other two are reported as no gain. Ids gate as for A.
* **D** (informational, pre-registered before `ab4`): k3 against k2 is measured as `base3` vs `base` and `fp8k3` vs `fp8` (4k, 3 rounds). No pass/fail: it is the recipe flip for the morning, reported with the paired ratio and the acceptance.
* **D-old** is not tonight's to flip; it goes to the morning decisions with the measured ratio.

## 3. Candidate B: block granularity of the MXFP4 GEMV (microbench, fleetq #8920): no effect

`scripts/c27/wpb/bench.cu` (copies of the one-row and the multi-row kernel with warps per block as a template parameter; 7 shapes of the 27B, M = 1 and 3, 64 rotating weight copies, best of 5 blocks of 60 launches;
`raw/wpb.log`). Warps per block 1, 2, 4, 8 (production), 16: every shape is within -3.4 % .. +2.5 % of the production launch at every width (gate/up M=3: 201.1 / 203.7 / 202.6 / 202.6 / 208.4 us against 202.3 for production; down M=3: 211.8 .. 220.0 against 217.0),
and every variant is bitwise the production output. The one outlier is the 1024-row k/v shape (16 us, launch bound): 13 to 15 % faster than the production launch at M = 1 at every width including the production 8, so that gap is the harness against the production launcher, not block size.
The launch tail is not a block's duration; rule B (>= 3 %) fails, nothing was built. Summing the microbench's M=3 times over one pass's launches (gate/up 128, down 64, qkv 48, z 48, out/o 64, q 16, k/v 32) gives 57.0 ms of body GEMV against 63.1 ms in the engine (section 1), 3 % of it
the in-graph gate/up and down kernels being slower than hot (208 against 202 us for gate/up); the remaining ~5 ms is not isolated (dependency waits and stream overlap are the candidates; no experiment was run).

## 7. Candidate E: captured verify attention as one row-table launch (`INTARSIA_VERIFY_ATTN_ROWS_CAPTURED=1`, fleetq #8951, window `raw/ab3/`): no gain, not kept

Implementation (commits `bd6f262b0`, then the per-capture-table commit; both reverted in the final tree): the eager verify pass already runs each layer's rows as one row-table launch (`verify_attn.rs`, lane vat); the captured MTP step keeps per-row launches. The opt-in let
the capture use the row-table launch with the step's device position table and its own K/V address table (the shared region of the eager path is rewritten by every eager pass and cannot be read by a graph that replays later).
A first version that read the shared region fell back to per-row launches inside the capture (the region was not filled before the capture; speed identical to base, 29.73 vs 29.75 on short-0), which is why the second version writes a per-capture table before the capture starts.
The captured engagement note `engaged in the captured step` was in stderr of the arm (`raw/ab3/ids/ids-short-0-capt.err`).

| size | base tok/s (3 rounds) | capt tok/s | paired ratios | median |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 4k | 28.81 28.78 28.82 | 28.49 28.50 28.38 | 0.989 0.990 0.985 | **0.989** |
| 16k | 25.01 25.08 25.04 | 25.40 25.51 25.43 | 1.016 1.017 1.016 | **1.016** |

Rule E (>= +2.0 % at 4k and >= +4.0 % at 16k): **FAIL at both**. Ids equal `--spec off` at short-0, 4k-1 and 16k-4 (`raw/ab3/ids.log`: 1433 / 1425 / 1411 B, md5 `7179d861` / `6d3eb261` / `865bbb3c`, base and capt). So the row-table launch is bitwise as claimed and slightly slower at 4k;
at 16k it saves 0.4 tok/s of 25. The L2 sharing the rule counted on is not there: the persistent kernel's item order does not put the three rows of one (kv head, split) next to each other in time (hypothesis, the item order was read, not traced).
What would still close the 16k attention gap (16.0 ms of 100.9 ms a pass, K/V read 3 times) is a kernel that streams K/V once for the three rows with the three rows' split partitions kept identical to the solo launch's: not tonight.

## 11. Morning decisions (nothing below is flipped)

1. **`INTARSIA_MTP_QUANT=fp8` as the 27B default** (recipe `[tuning]` or the loader default): +4.1 to +4.5 % MTP k2 decode at 1k / 4k / 16k, greedy output unchanged in every id gate, acceptance within a point. It changes the draft numerics (not the verified output), so under the project rule it is the user's call; sampled speculation (`match` / `reject`) was not measured with the quantised head.
   `mix` is the same call with +1.9 % more at 4k; `mxfp4` is not recommended (acceptance loss on short-1).
2. **k3 instead of k2** (recipe `spec_k`): +5.9 % (4k), +5.2 % (1k) over k2 with the fp8 head, **-0.4 % at 16k**. A context-dependent k (3 below about 8k, 2 above) would take the short end without the 16k loss; `--spec auto` is the existing mechanism and was not re-tuned.
