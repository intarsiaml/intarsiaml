## What was built (lever 1)

`INTARSIA_CUDA_GDN_VERIFY_FUSE` (unset = on, `=0` off, in `docs/tuning.md`). A speculative-verify GatedDeltaNet block ran its rows one at a time: per row `gdn_conv_kernel`, `gdn_delta_dec_kernel`, `gdn_conv_state_kernel`
and a copy of the whole recurrent slot into the row's rollback buffer (a rejected draft restores it). Now: `gdn_conv_snap_kernel` (one thread per channel, history in registers, rows in order, the conv state of every
row but the last written to its save) and `gdn_delta_snap_kernel` (the decode kernel's arithmetic looped over the rows with the delta-rule state in registers, bf16 states rounded at the end of every row as each one-row
launch rounds at its store, saves written in the same launch). `kernels/cuda/gdn.cu`, `ik_gdn_verify_snap_bf16` (`ik_cuda_abi.h`, `ffi.rs`), `Backend::gdn_verify_rows` (default `Ok(false)`: unsupported, nothing ran),
call sites in `model/gdn_block.rs` for the solo and the multi-sequence (`gdn_block_segs`) verify paths. Not a registered op, so the plan is untouched. Refuses (row by row, as before) for 1 row, more than 8 rows,
dk != 128, `INTARSIA_GDN_DEC=0`, `INTARSIA_CUDA_GDN_STATE_BF16_EMU=1`, a recorder (plan dry-run) installed, and misaligned slots.

Why 1.6 ms and not the 4.4 ms of GDN wall: the fused delta kernel still runs the rows serially per value row, and the 30 layers' conv + delta + state work is dependent; only the launch gaps, the 120 slot copies
(0.48 ms) and the per-row state round trips are gone. A nsys of the fuse binary was not taken (not needed for the verdict); the remaining GDN share is therefore an estimate (about 2.2 ms of wall).
