# mgdnv27: evidence gaps of the Metal fused GatedDeltaNet verify rows (`INTARSIA_METAL_GDN_VERIFY_FUSE=1`): the 27B, batched serving, sampled requests

Lane `mgdnv27`, branch `mgdnv27` from origin/mgdnv (PR #443 head, now merged to main as `533843814`). **No engine change**: the measured binary is PR #443's code, opt-in knob, nothing default-on. The knob is the user's call; this doc only fills the gaps listed in `docs/results/2026-11-18-mgdnv.md` ("Not verified").
Measured on endor (Apple M5 Max, macOS 27.0), tp 1, every run in a `fleetq` slot (`--quiet gpu,cpu`). Raw output: `docs/results/2026-11-18-mgdnv27/raw/`, scripts: `scripts/mgdnv27/` (cells and `cell.py` are mcopy's, `scripts/mcopy/`).

## Verdict

- **Exact on the 27B, batched serving and sampled requests: yes, in every comparison made.** 27B (Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4, default recipe = MTP k2): greedy ids knob on = knob off = `--spec off` in 5 of 5 cells, and in the 16-row-chain arm (`INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY=20,15`) in 2 of 2 cells. 35B batched (`--max-batch 4` and `16`, batched MTP verify): reply shas equal in 72 of 72 (round, request) pairs (24 + 48; 80 of 80 with the N = 2 no-drafting run). 35B sampled (temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95, fixed seeds): ids equal in 8 of 8 `generate` cells and 8 of 8 served replies.
- **Faster, modestly, except batched 16 which is noisy.** 27B ABBA (3 rounds x 3 reps, 4 cells): medians of the per-round ratios 1.014x to 1.032x, 11 of 12 cell-rounds >= 1.003 (the exception is chat-short round 1, 0.973, the first cell of an arm that started at thermal level 2). 35B aggregate tok/s, 6 rounds: `--max-batch 4` x 4 streams **1.052x** (every round 1.02 to 1.09), `--max-batch 16` x 8 streams **1.035x** with a spread of 0.908 to 1.229 (see below). 35B sampled batched, 1 round: 0.981x (not distinguishable from 1).
- **Default-on assessment: see the last section.** The decision is the user's; nothing was flipped here.

## What was measured, and how

Binary: `target-mgdnv27/intarsia-new` (`scripts/mgdnv27/build.sh`, release, `--features metal`), arms differ by env only. Prompts and cells: mcopy's (`scripts/mcopy/prompts.py`).

1. **27B** `~/models/Qwen3.8-27B-Quark-AWQ-MXFP4` (the model the Metal 27B docs, `recipes/qwen3_5_vl.toml` `[tuning.metal]` and `docs/results/2026-11-17-mherd.md` use; `~/models/qwen38-27b` is the bf16 original). It **has GatedDeltaNet layers** (`layer_types` with `linear_attention`) and the Metal recipe default is `--spec mtp --spec-k 2`, so the fused verify rows apply: the counter build (below) saw 5100 fused calls in a 300-token run, 5088 of them 3-row (MTP) calls. Arms of the ids gate: `--spec off`; the recipe default (no `--spec` flag); default + knob. For the two copy cells also `INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY=20,15` with and without the knob (16-row chain passes).
2. **35B batched** `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4`: `serve --max-batch MB --spec mtp --spec-k 2 --spec-off-at 9` with `INTARSIA_BATCHED_VERIFY=1` and `INTARSIA_BATCHED_MTP=N` (N = the number of concurrent requests; both are off by default on Metal, the recipe's N is 1). N matters: the first run used N = 2 with 4 streams and **never drafted** (1 fused call in the counter build, aggregate 247.4 vs 247.5 tok/s: kept as `raw/batch-mb4-bmtp2`, it is a no-op by construction, not evidence). Waves: N concurrent greedy requests (rotation chat-short / code-gen / reason / agent-edit, 300 tokens max), 2 waves per arm per round after a warm-up, a fresh server per arm, arm order reversed on even rounds (ABBA), 6 rounds per configuration, each round its own `fleetq` slot. Aggregate tok/s = sum of completion tokens / wall of the wave; the ratio is the median of the per-round ratios.
3. **35B sampled**: (a) `generate --temperature 0.7 --top-p 0.95 --seed S` at the default recipe (single sequence, sampled speculation `match`: verify passes ran, acceptance 68 to 100 %), S = 42 on chat-short, code-gen, reason, json-xform and S = 7 on chat-short, code-gen, reason, copy3k; knob on vs off ids. (b) served `--max-batch 16`, N = 8, temperature 0.7, per-request seeds 1234 + i, 1 round.

## 1. 27B (default recipe, MTP k2)

Ids (`raw/ids`, `raw/ids27c`, `ids.sh`; ids = `generate --chat --no-think --temperature 0 --show-ids`): **7 of 7 cells PASS, `ids fails=0` in both runs**: chat-short 326 ids, code-gen 500, reason 500, copy3k 2902, json-xform 2600 (arms def and def+on equal `--spec off` and each other); copy3k and json-xform with `INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY=20,15` (`copy`, `copy+on`) equal `--spec off` as well (2902 and 2600 ids). Ratios inside about 2 % are not distinguishable from noise on their own; the same-sign pattern across cells is the evidence.

(Reply sha equality is per round and request over both waves; the raw tables are `raw/tables-batch.md`.) The `--max-batch 16` ratio spreads over 0.908 to 1.229: the knob-off arm of round 1 ran at 200 tok/s (the later rounds' knob-off arm at 222 to 239), and round 4's knob-on arm was the slow one (215 against its usual 245). Both are single arm-runs the cooling wait (up to 120 s) did not explain (every server started at thermal level 0); no arm was dropped. Read the 16-slot result as "not slower in 5 of 6 rounds, +3.5 % median, spread too wide to quote a figure"; the 4-slot result is consistent (all six rounds above 1.02).
`--max-batch 4` with the recipe's N = 2 (`raw/batch-mb4-bmtp2`, 2 rounds): 1.000x, 8 of 8 shas equal, no drafting at 4 streams (see above).

## 3. 35B sampled requests

Sampling is seeded and deterministic here, so the comparison is possible: with a fixed `--seed` / request `seed` the sampler's RNG draws are the same sequence, and the fused rows are bitwise the row-by-row rows, so the accepted tokens do not move.
- `generate`, default recipe (MTP, sampled speculation `match`), temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95: seed 42 on chat-short (421 ids), code-gen (500), reason (500), json-xform (2600); seed 7 on chat-short (383), code-gen (500), reason (500), copy3k (2902): **8 of 8 PASS, ids knob on == knob off** (`raw/sampled`, `ids.sh` with `SAMPLED=1`). The verify passes ran in these cells (acceptance 68.3 %, 88.3 %, 92.0 %, 100 %, seed 42).
- served `--max-batch 16`, 8 streams, N = 8 (batched sampled MTP, `INTARSIA_BATCHED_MTP_SAMPLED` on by default), per-request seeds: **8 of 8 reply shas equal**, aggregate 204.5 off against 200.7 on (0.981x, 1 round, within the noise of section 2: not a measurement).
- Not checked: sampled requests with `reject` speculation (`INTARSIA_SPEC_SAMPLED=reject`), thinking-mode sampled output, other temperatures.

## Not verified

- One machine, one session; the batched ratios swing by +-10 % between rounds (other work on endor's CPUs, thermal state); `--max-batch 16` has no usable figure beyond the sign and the median.
- 27B: the ids gates use MTP k2 with the recipe's defaults (draft head, MXFP4 W4A16); `--max-batch` > 1 and sampled requests on the 27B were not run (the 27B recipe's batching default is 16 and `--spec mtp` with batching is the same engine as the 35B's, but it was not measured here).
- Batched: 4 and 8 concurrent requests of 300 tokens (short), not 16 streams, no long contexts, no mixed prefill/decode arrivals; the batched MTP engine is itself opt-in on Metal (`INTARSIA_BATCHED_MTP`, recipe 1), so with the recipe's N = 1 the batched branch with 2 or more drafting sequences does not occur at all.
- Soak (long contexts, thinking mode), tp > 1, prefix-store restores with the knob, the 4-bit / fp8 35B variants, CUDA / HIP (unchanged).
- The counter build used for the path check is not the measured binary.

## Revert

Nothing to revert: the knob is off by default, this lane changes no engine code (`INTARSIA_METAL_GDN_VERIFY_FUSE` unset = row by row).

## Default-on assessment (the decision is the user's)

With the knob on, every comparison this lane could make came out bitwise equal to the knob-off path (27B greedy ids 7 of 7, 35B batched replies 80 of 80, sampled ids and replies 16 of 16), the fused path was shown to run on both models and in the batched verify branch, and the speed is a consistent small gain: +1.4 to +3.2 % on the 27B default recipe (the model the user has on Metal as the batched-16 default), about +2 to +3 % on the 35B single sequence (#443), +5 % at four batched streams, +3.5 % median at eight with a wide spread, and nothing measured that is slower beyond noise. The main residual risks are things not run: the 27B with `--max-batch` and sampling, long-context and thinking soak, and the batched-16 speed, whose spread says the gain there is not pinned down; the knob only changes dispatch count (the arithmetic is the one-row kernels' body), and the unit test is a bitwise gate with a live negative control, so a default-on flip looks low-risk on correctness, with a modest, not large, payoff outside copy-chain recipes (`INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY`, +8 to +10 % in #443). If the user wants it default-on, the cheap safeguards are a flip that keeps `INTARSIA_METAL_GDN_VERIFY_FUSE=0` as the opt-out and a soak of the 27B at `--max-batch 16` with sampling before merging.
