# M1: new-model onboarding drill, Qwen3.8-27B (AMD Quark MXFP4), 2026-10-21

Lane M1 of `docs/plans/2026-10-21-quota-burn-plan.md`. Branch `m1/qwen38-mxfp4-drill` from origin/main `4be7b7d4`.
Claim under test (`docs/adding-a-model.md`): a new model gets a basic test within 1-2 h of its weights landing.
Model: the one production radiance serves on tatooine (`/data/models/rad/qwen3.8-27b-mxfp4.rad`). Its original checkpoint
is `amd/Qwen3.8-27B-Quark-AWQ-MXFP4` (rev `5233554c`, 19.8 GB, one `model.safetensors`). All times are EDT, from a start at
09:08:33.

## 1. Result

* **The claim holds, even with a new quant format.** The first correct generation on the R9700 came at **0h52m**
  (10:00:06, "The capital of France is Paris."). The first complete `qualify` quick tier, with every non-accuracy gate
  passing, came at **0h56m**. The first accuracy PASS came at **1h35m** (10:43:53): W4A16 against an HF W4A16 golden,
  21-way KL 8.3e-4 decode, 99.4 % top-1. These times include implementing MXFP4, previously design-only:
  * the scheme entry, CPU reference and loader;
  * HIP and CUDA kernels;
  * real-tensor tests;
  * one engine fix (the dense FFN loaded bf16 only);
  * a new golden tool, because no documented path could make a golden.
* **The checkpoint's declared mode (W4A4, dynamic MXFP4 activations) fails `acc-short`, and it cannot pass.** The failure
  is in the reference, not the engine. The HF reference does not reproduce itself to the gate's tolerance: HF bf16 against
  HF f32, both W4A4, gives a 21-way KL of **0.197**. intarsia against HF bf16 gives 0.176, and the floor is 0.03. The
  difference sits in a tail of near-tie positions: the median KL is 7.6e-3 for HF against HF and 7.7e-3 for intarsia
  against HF. Section 5 has the matrix.
* **Speed (not like-for-like).** intarsia HIP runs TP1 at 15.2 tok/s decode. radiance runs TP2 with 7 speculative tokens and
  fp8 KV at 144 tok/s (section 6).
* **Caveat on "weights landing".** The checkpoint was already on the fleet, in radiance's HF cache. A fresh download would add
  about 11 min for 19.8 GB at the ~30 MB/s seen copying tatooine to endor.

## 3. Architecture diff vs the supported families

`config.json` declares `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration` with a `qwen3_5` text config. The model is dense, not MoE, with
64 layers: 48 GatedDeltaNet and 16 gated full-attention, in the `qwen3_5` pattern. It also has one MTP layer (bf16) and a
vision tower (333 tensors). Compared with `recipes/qwen3_5.toml`:

* **New:** the multimodal wrapper. The text weights sit under `model.language_model.*`. Recipes have no prefix or
  inheritance, so `recipes/qwen3_5_vl.toml` is a prefixed copy (friction 4). Vision and MTP are not loaded: text-only, no
  speculation.
* **Unchanged:** the attention (gated, partial rope), the zero-centred RMSNorm (`norm_offset`), GDN and the FFN shapes.
  `builtin:qwen3_5_vl` covers 45 of 45 tensor templates.
* **Unverified:** `output_gate_type: "swish"`. HF transformers 5.19 does not read it and intarsia does not read it; both use
  a sigmoid gate. intarsia agrees with HF at 8e-4 KL in W4A16, so the two are at least consistent with each other.
* **Quant:** AMD Quark (`quant_method = "quark"`), OCP MXFP4. Weights are e2m1 with one e8m0 scale per 32 along K, and
  activations are quantised at run time with Quark's `even_round` scale (W4A4). `lm_head`, MTP and vision are excluded.
  This is "AWQ": the smoothing is already folded into the weights.

## 4. MXFP4 as a real quant format

* **Core** (`intarsia-core`):
  * `quant/mxfp4.rs`: format, detection, load, the CPU dequant reference and the activation QDQ (Quark `even_round`, e2m1
    nearest-even with saturation at 6), plus the effective activation mode.
  * `Op::MatmulMxfp4`, with checks, shapes and admission cases (`quant/cases.rs`), and the oracle in `quant/reference.rs`.
  * The Quark scheme parser in `quant/scheme.rs`: `MXFP4_W4A4` by default as declared; `--mxfp4-act none` opts into W4A16
    and is reported as a deviation.
* **Loader** (`intarsia-models/src/load.rs`): reads `quantization_config`, logs the effective activation mode, and gives
  quantised dense-FFN projections a QTensor (`model.rs` `proj()`).
* **CPU tests:**
  * `intarsia-backend-cpu/tests/quant_mxfp4.rs` checks the dequant and the QDQ bitwise against `tools/gen_mxfp4_fixture.py`
    on real tensor slices of this checkpoint (an independent numpy decode).
  * `intarsia-models/tests/hybrid_mxfp4.rs`: a tiny hybrid model in W4A16 matches its dequantised f32 copy (rel l2 < 1e-5);
    W4A4 stays finite.
  * `quark_scheme.rs` reads the real `config.json` as a fixture.
* **HIP** (`kernels/hip/mxfp4.hip`): GEMV and MMA. The activations are QDQ'd once into scratch. `kernels test`: ADMITTED,
  rel l2 ~9e-5.
* **CUDA** (`kernels/cuda/mxfp4.cu`): GEMV for any M. `kernels test` on spark1: 53 cases, 0 failures, ADMITTED.
* **Metal:** in progress (section 8).
* **Independence of the weight path.** The golden tool's weight dequant matches amd-quark's own unpack by inspection:
  `quark/torch/utils/pack.py` `Pack_mxfp4.unpack` (low nibble first, the same exponent bit trick) and
  `export/nn/modules/realquantizer.py:143` (scale `2^(e8m0 - 127)`). The tool did not execute Quark's code; `quark.torch`
  does not import without scipy and more. The W4A16 PASS and the coherent generations rule out a shared misreading of the
  layout in practice.
* **Sharding:** MXFP4 refuses to shard, so intarsia runs TP1 only.

## 5. Accuracy diagnosis (teacher-forced, 10 prompts, 1441 positions, 21-way KL)

The first two rows come from `intarsia accuracy` on the R9700 (prefill chunk 256). The rest come from
`golden_kl.py` (the same 21-way KL; 0.176 here against 0.180 from the gate for the same pair).

| reference | compared | KL mean | KL median | top-1 | gate (decode) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| HF W4A16 bf16 | intarsia W4A16 | 1.4e-3 (gate: 8.3e-4 decode) | 1.1e-5 | 99.4 % (decode) | **PASS** |
| HF W4A4 bf16 | intarsia W4A4 | 0.176 (gate: 0.180 prefill / 0.274 decode) | 7.7e-3 | 91.3 % / 88.8 % | FAIL |
| HF W4A4 bf16 | HF W4A4 **f32** | **0.197** | 7.6e-3 | ~90 % | (would FAIL) |
| HF W4A4 f32 | intarsia W4A4 | 0.226 | 8.3e-3 | | |
| HF W4A16 | HF W4A4 | 0.140 | 3.4e-3 | | |
| HF W4A16 | intarsia W4A4 | 0.137 | 5.5e-3 | | |

Reading:
* The W4A16 row validates the weights, the loader, GDN, attention and the FFN in intarsia.
* In W4A4 the reference moves more between its own bf16 and f32 runs than intarsia does from it. Rounding activations to fp4
  turns last-bit differences into whole e2m1 steps at near-ties, so each precision draws its own quantisation noise.
* Neither W4A4 run is closer to W4A16 than the other: 0.140 for HF, 0.137 for intarsia.
* The absolute 3e-2 floor is below this reference noise. No engine can pass it in W4A4 unless it is bit-identical to the
  golden.

Not run: engine self-noise in W4A4 (chunk 64 against a chunk-256 self-golden). `--self-golden` refuses a self-golden from a
build without `INTARSIA_BUILD_STAMP` (friction 18).

## 7. Friction list

Engine and tooling. Items marked F are fixed on this branch.

1. F: `recipe new` classified the MXFP4 tensors as `unknown(weight_scale next to a U8 weight)` and refused Quark's
   `quant_method = "quark"`.
2. F: `recipe new` chose the closest recipe by tensor-template overlap, `qwen3_5_moe` (another family), and took the
   model-level fields from it. `norm_offset` (zero-centred RMSNorm) and the parsers became TODOs, although `qwen3_5` is this
   model's own family. The fields now come from a same-family recipe.
3. F: the default output of `recipe new` is `recipes/<family>.toml`, which here is the family's existing recipe. The error
   told the user to pass `--force`, which overwrites another model's recipe. It now writes to the first free
   `recipes/<family>_<n>.toml` and prints the path.
4. Recipes have no prefix parameter or inheritance. The VL wrapper needs a full copy of `qwen3_5.toml` with
   `model.language_model.` prefixed (`recipes/qwen3_5_vl.toml`).
5. F: the dense FFN block loaded bf16 only. A quantised dense FFN failed in `plan` with "tensor ... has shape
   [17408, 2560], expected [17408, 5120]" (the packed fp4 width), which does not say "quantised FFN unsupported".
6. No golden could be made through the documented paths:
   * radiance ignores `prompt_logprobs`;
   * the fleet has no bf16 base;
   * `tools/golden.py` loads the full model (54 GB bf16) and needs amd-quark, whose `quark.torch` import needs scipy and more;
   * `--make-golden-max-gb` is 5.

   F (tool): `tools/golden_quark_mxfp4.py`.
7. **The checkpoint's declared W4A4 mode always FAILs `acc-short`** (section 5): the absolute class floor does not allow for
   the reference's own noise in activation-quantised schemes. Documented in `adding-a-model.md` (gate in W4A16; quote W4A4
   against a second, f32 golden). Not changed: see section 8.
8. `qualify --resume` keeps FAIL stages, and the stages they blocked. After the FFN fix a new `--out` was needed. Documented.
9. `intarsia qualify --out` cannot be repeated (a wrapper script that sets `--out` cannot be overridden on its command line).
10. Metal has no GatedDeltaNet or quantised-matmul kernels, so no Qwen3.5-family model runs on Metal. The CPU backend's quant
    and GDN ops are f64 references, so a 27B CPU smoke test needs ~30 GB and runs very slowly. The docs' "cpu first" fallback
    does not work for this model.
11. MXFP4 refuses to shard, so intarsia runs TP1 only, while radiance runs TP2.
12. `output_gate_type: "swish"` in the config is read by neither HF transformers 5.19 nor intarsia. Both use a sigmoid gate.
    Unverified risk.

Fleet and process:

13. Finding the original checkpoint: radiance serves a converted `.rad`. The HF original turned up only through unanimis
    (`unim recall`) and radiance's HF cache. The other two 27B checkpoints on the fleet are not usable: RadixArk NVFP4
    (mixed NVFP4 W4A4 + FP8) is unsupported, and the other is an unsloth GGUF.
14. `fleetq --takes-down radiance` marks radiance down but does not stop the unit. The first diagnosis attempt ran out of
    device memory with radiance still running. The unit has to be stopped and started by hand, with `FLEETQ_JOB` set on the
    command itself (the fleetq guard blocks it otherwise).
15. `deploy/tatooine/dev.sh` has no host networking, so localeval cannot reach a server started in it. A separate
    `docker run --network host` was needed.
16. Slot contention: every GPU step waited behind other lanes' windows, and the CPU goldens waited behind exclusive Metal
    benchmarks on endor.
17. macOS rsync has no `--info=progress2`, so the first copy attempt failed. 19.8 GB from tatooine to endor took ~11 min
    (~30 MB/s).
18. `accuracy --self-golden` refuses a self-golden written by a build without `INTARSIA_BUILD_STAMP`, and the ad-hoc tatooine
    builds carry no stamp. The error appears only after the model has loaded.
19. localeval's unanimis capture failed for both speed runs ("Session open refused by peer", ssh mux).

## 8. What is left

* **Gate semantics (proposal, not implemented).** Let the absolute floor take a measured reference spread, for example
  `--golden-noise-ref <second golden>`, with floor = max(class floor, k x KL(ref, ref2)). Alternatively gate on the median
  plus a tail check for activation-quantised schemes. Either change alters pass/fail for every model and backend, so it is
  the coordinator's call. Section 5 is the data for it.
* **Metal MXFP4 GEMV kernel:** in progress on this branch (`kernels/metal/mxfp4.metal`). It is a kernel-test-only
  deliverable, because Metal still lacks GDN and cannot run this model.
* **localeval quality head-to-head** (mmlu, gsm8k) between intarsia W4A4 and radiance: needs ~25 min of radiance downtime,
  with a slot window per engine.
* **Speed:** a tuned MXFP4 GEMV on HIP, and TP2 sharding for MXFP4.
* **Unused parts of the checkpoint:** MTP (speculation) and the vision tower are not loaded.
