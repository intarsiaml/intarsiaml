## What I built

1. **`kernels/hip/moe_wmma_r7.h`** (new; `moe_wmma.h` untouched), `moe_wmma_kernel_r7<GATEUP, PAIRS, V>`.
   It is `moe_wmma_kernel_v` plus three variant bits:
   * **kMwCvt (8): fp4 decode without the LDS table.**
     * Each e2m1 nibble's sign and 3 magnitude bits move into an e4m3 byte, `(s << 7) | (mag << 2)`, whose value is exactly
       e2m1 x 2^-6. Magnitude 1 becomes the e4m3 subnormal 2^-7, and the largest byte is 0x9c, never NaN.
     * That takes 7 integer ops per 8 weights (even and odd nibbles into two words). `v_cvt_pk_f32_fp8` converts them two
       at a time.
     * The block scale is folded with 2^6 (`sc * 64`, exact). `(v * 2^-6) * (sc * 64)` is the exact product `v * sc`, the
       same f32 bits as the table's `lut[c] * sc`, sign of zero and NaN scales included.
     * bf16 is packed by `v_perm`, as before. The bf16 fragments and every WMMA are bit for bit the round-6 ones.
   * **kMwTwo (16), down only: two A fragments per wave.** That gives 256 features per block, so each B fragment read
     from LDS feeds two WMMAs. Every output still accumulates the same WMMAs over k in the same order.
   * **kMwDeep (32): six weight blocks in flight per lane** instead of three.
2. **Per-projection selection** (`moe.hip`).
   * `INTARSIA_HIP_MOE_MW=v|g:d`: `v` for both projections, or `g` for gate_up and `d` for down.
   * Accepted values: 0 | 1 | 4 | 5 (round 5/6) and 13 | 25 | 29 | 41 | 45 (round 7). Anything else falls back to the
     tile's default.
   * New defaults, from W2 / W3:

     | tile | gate_up | down |
     |---|---|---|
     | 64 pairs | 45 = cvt + skip + occupancy + deep | 25 = cvt + skip + two fragments |
     | 32 pairs | 0, the round-5 kernel | 41 = cvt + skip + deep |

   * The launch is now one switch per projection (`moe_wmma_launch_p`).
3. **Pin**: `ik_hip_moe_mw_down(pairs)` (`ik_hip_abi.h`, `ffi.rs`) and `moe_down_variants()` (`lib.rs`), next to round 6's
   `inner_variants()`.
4. **Tests.**
   * `tests/moe_pairs.rs` runs every value at both tiles against the round-5 kernel, the no-knob defaults, and three `g:d`
     splits.
   * New cases: every finite e4m3 scale byte (zero, subnormals, negatives; `random_rows` only draws 0x30..0x41), NaN
     scale bytes, h = 320 (the 256-feature down tile runs past the last row), and i = 192 (three k blocks, fewer than the
     deep ring).
   * `tests/round6_selection.rs` pins both projections' defaults at both tiles, every accepted value, `g:d` with empty
     and invalid halves, and rejected values, including the dropped variants.
5. **Scripts.**
   * `remote.sh`: stages `r7test`, `mutbuild7` / `mutrun7`, `pf35one`; `pfprofenv` takes several `NAME=value`.
   * `mutate.py`: 19 new `r7-*` entries; main's entries are untouched.

## What I tried and dropped

Measured in W1-W3 (4408-token prefill profiles; `raw/prof/moe-profiles-summary.txt`; base gate_up<64> 97-98 ms, down<64>
52 ms in those windows):

* **Two fragments in gate_up** (the gate and up rows of the same 16 features in one wave, silu*up in registers, no up
  accumulators in LDS). Variant 25 (W1): gate_up<64> **125.8 ms** against 88.9 for 13, and gate_up<32> 64.5 against 30.4.
  Slower; the bit is now down-only.
* **kMwCvt alone** (8): gate_up<64> 97.8 ms, no gain. With occupancy (13): 87.9. With skip, no occupancy (9): 91.6. The
  conversion pays off only together with kMwOcc's 8 waves.
* **Block scales through the fp8 conversion too** (`kMwScv`, 93 / 125). 93: gate_up 86.4 against 85.8 for 61, no gain.
  125 spilled (16 / 28 bytes scratch). Removed.
* **Deep ring in the two-fragment down** (61 for down): 256 VGPRs, 8 bytes scratch, down<64> 48.6 against 45.5 for 25/29.
* **Deep ring without skip / occupancy** (40): gate_up 95.3 ms.
* **r7 variants at gate_up<32>**: 13 gives 40.4 ms and 41 gives 31.9, against 30.1-30.4 for the round-5 kernel. Kept the
  round-5 kernel.
* **MoE grid z** (`INTARSIA_HIP_MOE_Z` 0 / 4 / 8 x variants 5 / 61 / 29, W3): the default (2) was best or equal in every
  pairing. The MoE projections are not tail-bound.
* From the brief:
  * *Block scale once per fragment*: not bitwise. The scale differs per 16 weights along k, inside one WMMA's k sum, so
    it cannot come out of the sum without changing the bf16 products. Not done.
  * *Scale loads out of LDS*: the scales already came from global memory into registers; the LDS traffic was the fp4 table,
    now gone.
  * *Explicit dequant-ahead software pipelining*: not built as a separate variant. kMwDeep deepens the global-load ring;
    the dequant of fragment s+1 is left to the compiler's schedule inside the unrolled s loop.
  * *Magic-number / `v_perm` fp4 -> bf16 tricks*: replaced by the hardware fp8 conversion, which builds the f32 directly.
