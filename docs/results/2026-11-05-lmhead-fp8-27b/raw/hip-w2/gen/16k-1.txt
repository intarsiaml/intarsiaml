

This document is a **technical specification and implementation log** for adding a new speculative decoding method called **DFlash** (and its enhancement, **DCOCO**) to the `intarsia` inference engine.

Here is a detailed breakdown for a new engineer, structured by context, architecture, implementation, and results.

### 1. Context & Goal
*   **The Problem:** Standard autoregressive decoding (generating one token at a time) is slow. Speculative decoding speeds this up by using a smaller "draft" model to guess multiple tokens at once, which a larger "target" model then verifies in parallel
