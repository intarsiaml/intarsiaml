# mr: P0 ablation of the row-exact MXFP4 GEMV on HIP (lane mr, 2026-10-25)

Branch `lane-mr/rows2-scope`. Measurement only, standalone harness (`scripts/mr/mxp0.hip`), no production code changed. Question from
docs/plans/2026-10-25-mr-hip-mxfp4-rows2-scope.md: would a bitwise-safe kernel change (A: x pre-converted to f32, B: two activation rows per
barrier group) make the 2-weight-row `mxfp4_gemv_rows_kernel` at least 10 % faster at M=16 on both big shapes?

## Result: gate failed, stop

| gate (M=16, both 17408x5120 and 5120x17408) | result |
|---|---|
| A (f32 x) at least 10 % faster | NO: 311 vs 272 us (+14 %) and 310 vs 275 us (+13 %) |
| A bitwise equal to the M=1 launch of each row | yes, 0 mismatches at M=4, 8, 12, 16, all three shapes |
| B (2 rows per barrier) | NO: 289 vs 272 us (+6 %), 289 vs 275 us (+5 %), bitwise |
| A+B | NO: 335 and 393 us (+23 %, +43 %), bitwise |

Why A loses: f32 x doubles the x bytes read by every warp (each warp walks all M rows of x), and the kernel is not limited by VALU issue.
A removes 28 % of the static VALU (1684 to 1214 at M=16) and raises fma+fma pairing from 13 % to 64 %, and it still gets slower.

## What the numbers say

* The most any x-side change can win is the ALU floor: 13 % (17 % at M=8) on the big shapes, 20 % on 5120x6144. A real kernel cannot reach it
  (x must be loaded and unpacked); the same-group variant, which only removes L0 misses, gets 4 to 5 %.
* ALU floor slope: 13.1 us per extra activation row on 17408x5120 (89.1M weights), i.e. 6.8 T fma/s = 13.6 TFLOP/s fp32, with 77 % of the fmas in
  dual instructions. That is far below the 47.8 TFLOP/s dual-issue peak used for the 18 ms bound (R9700 clock assumed 2.9 GHz, not measured).
  At 13.6 TFLOP/s the fma work of 16 rows over 27e9 weights is about 63 ms, so the row-exact fp32 chain structure caps the 83 ms GEMV figure near
  63 ms, and the 18 ms bound is not reachable by this kernel family.
* Dual-issue share does not predict speed here: variant 1 pairs 64 % of its fmas and is slower than prod (13 %). This undercuts lever B.
* Corrections to earlier figures (also noted in docs/results/2026-10-25-mr-hip-logits-rows.md): the "57 % dual-issued" counted fmas sharing a
  dual instruction with any op; only 13 % pair with another fma. The bf16 unpack is about 200 VALU, not 330 (the and/shift total includes ~134 fp4 index ops).

## Method

`scripts/mr/mxp0.hip` includes kernels/hip/mxfp4.hip, runs the production kernel and a template copy with the variants, x already QDQ-free (W4A16 input),
3 rotating weight copies, 200 calls x 3 rounds, reference = the production M=1 launch of each row. `scripts/mr/p0build.sh` builds it and the ISA
(`scripts/mr/p0isa.py` counts per kernel instance), `scripts/mr/p0win.sh` is the radiance-down window. fleetq jobs #5369/#5377 (build), #5388 (timing,
card 0). A first window (#5380) was wasted by a shell quoting bug (the harness got shapes as extra numeric arguments and ran 5120 x 5120 rounds); I stopped
its container, radiance came back on its own, the harness now rejects non-numeric arguments.

Not done: no rocprof counters (cache hit rates, VALU busy), so "not VALU-issue-bound" is inferred from the A result, not from a counter.
