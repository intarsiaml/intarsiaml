### 2. Loader / engine gaps (answers brief 2; re-verified on `9a6e28b82`)

- CUDA still refuses DFlash2 at load: `crates/intarsia-models/src/load.rs:854-868` (`matches!(backend_name, "cpu" | "hip" | "metal")` for v2 / DSpine).
- No CUDA `Op::DynConv` (the draft's grouped dynamic conv): `crates/intarsia-backend-cuda/src/kernels.rs:1034,1046` (`NOT_ON_CUDA`). HIP kernel to port.
- Block attention with a runtime window and split-KV window kernel are HIP-only (`model/dflash2.rs:394`, `device.rs:570-588`); device top-k never ran on CUDA (`dflash2.rs:173-176`).
- Reusable: hybrid GDN `SpecRows` rollback and KV rollback are shared with `--spec mtp` and run for the hybrid 27B on HIP; taps already work on GDN and attention layers. MoE tap glue (`model.rs:4034-4043`, pending-block add) has no MoE test.
- Product limits even when built: `--spec dflash` refuses the batching engine (`load.rs:851`, our CUDA default is batched MTP), macro prefill is off with a draft loaded (`model.rs:1503`), the prefix store is bypassed, and graph capture / deferred verify is MTP-only (`engine.rs:2116,2393,2412`), so every DFlash pass would be eager.
- Effort: dfl35 estimated 16 to 24 h (B1-B7 table in its doc), under the 2-day bar. Effort is not what stops the lane; the expected result is.

Source: `docs/results/2026-11-18-cvat.md` line 123, `docs/results/2026-11-18-copycuda.md` lines 41-56, `docs/results/2026-11-17-dfl35.md` section 3.

Our default MTP k2 beats TF's DFlash2 figure on every cell, by 1.06x (chat-short) to 1.25x (code-gen). Our MTP is 1.4 to 1.65x TF's MTP. TF's 1.31x is DFlash2 over a weaker MTP baseline and does not carry over.

Cross-check that TF's 130 is also what we would expect from our engine, not just a weak TF baseline: our plain step is 11.8 ms and an MTP pass about 16 ms at 2.45 tokens per pass (cmtp). A DFlash2 pass (7 drafted rows, about 8 to 10 ms draft plus an 8-row MoE verify touching about 60 distinct experts per layer, 25 to 30 ms; dfl35 bandwidth arithmetic, not measured) is about 38 to 42 ms, so 130 tok/s needs about 5.2 tokens per pass and matching MTP k2's 160 needs about 6.4. The 27B's DFlash2 gets 2.5 (prose) to 5.1 (code) tokens per pass (`2026-11-06-27b-dflash-hip-tp1.md`). So even at TF's acceptance the pass cost lands near 130, consistent with TF's number, and below our MTP. A tokens-per-pass of 6.4 or more on our cells would be needed to flip this; the only proxy we have says 2.5 to 5.1.

### 4. Go / no-go

NO-GO. Best case parity with MTP k2 on code-like text, a loss on prose, chat and reasoning (the same split as the 27B), at the cost of 16 to 24 h plus giving up batching, macro prefill, the prefix store and graph replay. Copy-heavy cells are already covered by the opt-in `INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY` drafts (1.7 to 2.06x).

Condition that would reopen it: a measured tokens-per-pass of at least 6.4 for this draft on this target on our code-gen / agent-edit cells. That needs no CUDA work if the draft runs on a backend that already accepts DFlash2 (HIP or CPU; `load.rs:854-868`): a `generate --spec dflash --spec-draft <35B draft dir>` run on a HIP box would print acceptance. Whether the 35B target's MoE tap glue works there is untested (B5 in the dfl35 doc), so this check could itself fail. It was not run here (probe-only lane, no hardware run).
