# samp64: why sampled decode at 64k falls behind vLLM with bf16 KV, and an opt-in context schedule for sampled drafting

Lane `samp64`, branch `samp64` from origin/main `a2c83ca47` (after PR #468), 2026-11-20, spark2, fleetq owner `claude-samp64`. Cutover blocker 5 of `docs/results/2026-11-19-ab35d-cutover-scoreboard.md`: the shipping configuration (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B NVFP4, CUDA, `serve --max-batch 2 --prefix-store on`, bf16 KV, MTP `--spec-k 2` by recipe, sampled drafting on) decodes sampled requests (T=0.7) at x1.27 of the production vLLM at 4k, x0.985 at 32k and x0.83 at 64k, while greedy decode is x1.53 / 1.32 / 1.13 at 4k / 16k / 32k. fp8 KV stays opt-in (user decision 2026-11-13) and is not touched here. vLLM was not re-measured: ab35d's A numbers (same box, same client script) are reused.

## Method

- Box and binary: spark2 with the production vLLM (`retro`) up and untouched; one `intarsia serve` per window, built from origin/main `a2c83ca47` (stamp `samp64-a2c83ca47aec`, fleetq #10381, `--load cpu`, 6 min), command line as ab35d's w1 except the port: `serve --backend cuda --model <nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4 snapshot 1355db6a> --max-seq 131072 --mem-budget 40 --profile-every 0 --max-batch 2 --prefix-store on` plus the window's change (`--spec off`, `--spec-k 1`, or an `INTARSIA_*` variable). Everything else default (bf16 KV, MTP k=2, batched MTP N=2, verify top-k, side-stream eager, CUDA verify-attention rows captured).
- Client: `scripts/ab35b/sampled.py` (ab35d's script: localeval's streaming `Client`, filler prompts with a nonce first so nothing is cached, exactly 256 tokens forced, one stream at a time, `top_p` 0.95, checkpoint `top_k` 20), here with 5 reps per size, sizes 4096 / 32768 / 65536 (real prompts 4.07k / 33.9k / 68.9k tokens), temperature 0.7 for "sampled" and 0 for "greedy" on the same prompts. MTP acceptance and tokens per verify pass come from the server's `/metrics` `intarsia_spec_*` counters diffed over each size's reps (`tokens per pass = (accepted + passes) / passes`).
- Windows (scripts in `scripts/samp64/`, one fleetq job per phase, `--quiet gpu,cpu`): w1 shipping config, w2 `--spec off`, w3 `--spec-k 1`, w4 `INTARSIA_SPEC_K_MIN=1`, w5 `INTARSIA_SPEC_COSTGATE=1`; each with sampled then greedy cells. Raw: `docs/results/2026-11-20-samp64/raw/` (`w<N>-T<temp>-sampled.{json,out}`, serve logs and `/metrics` before / after, clock samples, memory samples, window logs, `tables-phase12.md`).
- Clock guard (lane rules 2026-11-19): `nvidia-smi` sampled every 2 s for every window, the "SW Power Capping" counter read before and after every cell. No cell tripped it: minimum SM clock 2307 MHz at more than 90 % utilization, mean 2372 to 2379 MHz, SW power cap delta 0 us in every cell, maximum power 93 W. Lowest MemAvailable in any window 37.4 GB (kill line 15 GB, earlyoom active, 0 kills, 0 warn / error lines in any serve log).
- Window to window noise: the same configuration (w1 and w4 are different configurations, but the shipping config appears in several windows of this lane) moves by 2 to 5 % between server starts at 64k (52.0, 52.7, 55.0 tok/s for k=2 sampled), so gates below compare same-binary windows run back to back and repeat the baseline.

## Result 1: the sampled deficit at 64k is the draft policy, and greedy decode runs into the same wall

Shipping config, bf16 KV, single stream, decode tok/s, median of 5 reps (min-max), same prompts (real prompts 4.07k / 33.9k / 68.9k tokens):

| config | 4k | 32k | 64k |
|---|---|---|---|
| sampled T=0.7, MTP k=2 (shipping) | 95.4 (92-97) | 69.1 (68-73) | 52.0 (51-54) |
| sampled T=0.7, `--spec off` | 84.6 (83-85) | 69.5 (69-70) | **57.6** (57-58) |
| greedy, MTP k=2 (shipping) | 120.3 (116-146) | 81.3 (79-86) | 60.4 (60-63) |
| greedy, `--spec off` | 85.8 (86-89) | 71.7 (71-72) | 59.1 (59-60) |
| vLLM (ab35d, fp8 KV, no speculation), sampled | 77.7 | 71.7 | 65.3 |

- **Spec-off control: at 64k, sampled decode with drafting is 10 % slower than without it** (52.0 against 57.6 tok/s; the spec-off reps are tight, 57.3 to 57.9, the spec-on reps are not, 51.1 to 53.8; in other windows of this lane the same shipping config read 52.7 and 55.0). At 32k they tie (69.1 against 69.5) and at 4k drafting gains 13 % (95.4 against 84.6). So the drafting policy is the problem: past about 32k tokens of context a verify pass costs more rows of attention than the drafts it accepts save. ab35d's observation "MTP gains nothing at 64k" is right and is in fact a loss.
- **Greedy does not escape it, it only hits it later.** Greedy drafting gains +40 % at 4k, +13 % at 32k and +2 % at 64k over greedy spec-off. The sampled / greedy ratio is flat (0.79, 0.85, 0.86): sampled does not fall off faster than greedy. What ab35d's scoreboard compares at 64k is a sampled cell (x0.83) against greedy cells that stop at 32k (x1.13); greedy decode at 64k is 60.4 tok/s, x0.92 of vLLM's 65.3 (vLLM's sampled and greedy are alike), so greedy at 64k is behind vLLM too, by 8 %.
- **Attribution (derived from throughput and acceptance; the profiled version is in the next section).** ms per verify pass = tokens per pass x 1000 / tok/s; a plain step is the spec-off run's 1000 / tok/s.

| | 4k | 32k | 64k |
|---|---|---|---|
| sampled pass, ms (tokens per pass) | 22.3 (2.13) | 30.4 (2.10) | 40.1 (2.08) |
| sampled plain step, ms | 11.8 | 14.4 | 17.4 |
| sampled pass / plain step | 1.89 | 2.11 | 2.31 |
| greedy pass, ms (tokens per pass) | 18.9 (2.27) | 26.6 (2.17) | 36.8 (2.22) |
| greedy plain step, ms | 11.7 | 14.0 | 16.9 |
| greedy pass / plain step | 1.62 | 1.91 | 2.17 |

  A pass has to yield more tokens than its cost in plain steps to pay: sampled needs 1.89 / 2.11 / 2.31 and gets 2.13 / 2.10 / 2.08 (win, tie, loss); greedy needs 1.62 / 1.91 / 2.17 and gets 2.27 / 2.17 / 2.22 (win, small win, tie). Three things follow:
  1. **The KV cost of a pass scales with its rows.** From 4k to 64k a plain step grows by 5.6 ms (the 10 full-attention layers reading 20 KB of bf16 KV per token each step: 1.4 GB at 64k, 5 ms at the Spark's bandwidth) and a pass by 17.8 ms (sampled) or 17.9 ms (greedy), 3.2 times as much: a verify pass of 3 rows reads the cache once per row (`attn_decode_bf16_splitkv_rows`, one launch per layer but one K / V stream per row).
  2. **Acceptance does not move with context** (sampled 56.5 / 55.3 / 54.3 %, greedy 63.8 / 58.4 / 61.2 %), so the loss is not a drafting-quality effect: the same tokens per pass cost more as context grows.
  3. **A sampled pass carries a fixed overhead over a greedy pass of about 3.4 ms at every size** (22.3 against 18.9, 30.4 against 26.6, 40.1 against 36.8 ms; plain steps differ by 0.1 to 0.5 ms), which is also why sampled drafting gains less than greedy at 4k (+13 % against +40 %). Reading of the code, not measured here (see the profile): with one stream (`busy` is `b > 1`) a keyed (sampled) request drafts and verifies on its own eager path (`Seq::keyed_draft`: `Model::mtp_draft`, then `Model::forward_verify_logits` with whole logits rows and host draws), while a greedy request replays the captured `spec_step` graph; with two streams both take the deferred batched verify pass, with top-k rows for sampled ones.

## Result 2: existing knobs

All on the shipping config, sampled and greedy, same prompts (`raw/tables-phase12.md` has every cell and acceptance):

| sampled T=0.7, tok/s (acceptance, tokens per pass) | 4k | 32k | 64k |
|---|---|---|---|
| k=2 (shipping, w1) | 95.4 (56.5 %, 2.13) | 69.1 (55.3 %, 2.10) | 52.0 (54.3 %, 2.08) |
| `--spec-k 1` (w3) | 91.0 (73.2 %, 1.73) | 71.6 (71.9 %, 1.72) | 56.5 (71.0 %, 1.71) |
| `INTARSIA_SPEC_K_MIN=1`, adaptive depth 1 to 2 (w4) | 97.4 (65.5 %, 2.29) | 71.9 (57.1 %, 2.13) | 52.7 (57.8 %, 2.14) |
| `INTARSIA_SPEC_COSTGATE=1` (w5) | 94.2 | 70.7 | 55.0 |
| `--spec off` (w2) | 84.6 | 69.5 | 57.6 |

| greedy, tok/s | 4k | 32k | 64k |
|---|---|---|---|
| k=2 (shipping, w1) | 120.3 | 81.3 | 60.4 |
| `--spec-k 1` (w3) | 119.0 | 84.8 | 65.5 |
| `INTARSIA_SPEC_K_MIN=1` (w4) | 118.9 | 82.6 | 60.2 |
| `INTARSIA_SPEC_COSTGATE=1` (w5) | 115.3 | 81.4 | 59.1 |
| `--spec off` (w2) | 85.8 | 71.7 | 59.1 |

- **One draft (`--spec-k 1`) is the better depth at long context for both kinds of request**: sampled +8.7 % at 64k (56.5 against 52.0) and +3.6 % at 32k, but -4.6 % at 4k; greedy +8.4 % at 64k (65.5 against 60.4, x1.00 of vLLM) and +4.3 % at 32k, -1 % at 4k. A pass of two rows costs less KV than three and its first draft is accepted more often (71 to 79 % against 54 to 61 %).
- `INTARSIA_SPEC_K_MIN=1` (the existing adaptive depth) moves the 4k cell (+2 %) and nothing at 64k: its climb rule keeps the depth at 2 while two drafts are accepted often enough. `INTARSIA_SPEC_COSTGATE=1` never engages in the batching engine: `intarsia_spec_costgate_off_total`, `_on_total`, `_plain_steps_total` and `_probe_steps_total` read 0 after the whole window (`raw/serve-w5.metrics`; the gate documents itself as single-sequence only), so its row is a repeat of the shipping config, and a measure of the window-to-window noise (64k sampled 55.0 against 52.0 and 52.7).
