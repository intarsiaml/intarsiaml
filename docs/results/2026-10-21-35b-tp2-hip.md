# 35B tp=2 on 2× R9700 (HIP, lane G2b, 2026-10-21)

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B NVFP4 at tp=2 on tatooine's two R9700s: one process, one thread per rank, the HIP P2P all-reduce
(`crates/intarsia-backend-hip/src/comm.rs`). It runs on G1b's shared tp=2 core (`feat/35b-tp2`, PR #44) **with no
HIP-specific code change**. Branch `feat/35b-tp2-hip`, on origin/main after #44 merged. Raw logs: `2026-10-21-35b-tp2-hip/raw/`.
Accuracy dumps are not committed (`raw/.gitignore`); they stay in `tatooine:~/g2b/out/<window>/`.

## Headline

- **The HIP no-op of G1b's branch passes.** At tp=1 everything is identical to origin/main, on 8B and 35B:
  - `plan show`;
  - the accuracy dumps, bit for bit, and accuracy stdout;
  - 256 greedy ids on the short and 4k prompts.
  - The existing 8B HIP tp=2 path gives the same ids before and after the branch.
- **The 35B at tp=2 on HIP works out of the box.** It passes every gate but one:
  - the golden gate on both sets;
  - prefix reuse (aligned restore bit-identical);
  - long-set KL21 under the user's run-to-run bar, 0.93x;
  - the HIP crate's 92 GPU tests.
- **The one failure is the short-set KL21:** 1.65x vLLM's run-to-run (the bar is ≤ 1x).
  - tp=1 with fusion off is bitwise identical to tp=1 default on HIP. So the change comes from the sharded arithmetic
    itself, not from tp>1 turning fusion off.
- **Speed, tp=2 vs tp=1 on one card:**
  - prefill: +18 % to +27 %;
  - decode: −10 % to −17 %.
  - Against tp=1 with graphs and fusion off, which is what each tp=2 rank runs, decode is +13 % to +19 %. The decode
    loss comes from core turning HIP graphs and fusion off at tp>1.

- **Long: PASS.** The change is under vLLM's run-to-run on both re-runs.

  - On CUDA the same core sharding gives 1.06x. HIP's kernels at the halved shapes move the short set further.

## Decisions for the user

1. **The short-set KL21 at tp=2 on HIP is 1.65x vLLM's run-to-run; long is 0.93x.**
   - Is the short-set result acceptable for HIP tp=2? It would stay opt-in (`--tp 2`); the default is tp=1 and is
     unchanged.
   - Or should HIP tp=2 be held until the halved-shape kernels are matched? CUDA gives 1.06x on the same sharding.
2. **Graphs and fusion at tp>1 (a core policy since the TP phase):** they cost HIP tp=2 about 25 % of decode.
   - The HIP P2P all-reduce is one kernel launch with an on-device handshake, so it is capturable in principle.
   - On HIP, fusion is bitwise, so allowing row-local fusions under tp would not move numerics.
   - Both are core changes with sync and order risk, so they belong in a follow-up lane with mutants.
3. **Production use:** radiance (Qwen3.8-27B) already holds both cards at TP=2. For the 35B on tatooine, tp=2 buys
   prefill only, and costs decode unless (2) lands.
