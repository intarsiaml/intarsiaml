# mrows16: copy-draft chains of 8 to 15 tokens on Metal (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B NVFP4, endor)

Lane `mrows16`, branch `mrows16` from origin/main `6f7691bee` (PR #435, lane mcopym). Opt-in only: `INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY=<n>[,<len>]` beside `--spec mtp --spec-k 2`, off by default, default `len` stays 7.
Measured on endor (Apple M5 Max, macOS 27.0), `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4`, tp 1, every run in a `fleetq` slot (`--quiet gpu,cpu`). Raw output: `docs/results/2026-11-18-mrows16/raw/`; scripts: `scripts/mrows16/`
(mcopym's scripts with the new paths; cells and table script are mcopy's, `scripts/mcopy/`).

## Verdict

- **`len` 8..=15 now works on Metal, exact.** Fix (1) of the mcopym sizing, in a slightly different form (below). 10 of 10 greedy-id gate cells equal `--spec off` at `len` 15 and `len` 12; knob unset `plan show --json` byte-identical to origin/main; the new 9..=16-row bitwise tests pass.
- **Speed: `len` 15 is not a clear win over `len` 7, so the default stays 7 and this is the user's call.** n20,15 against n20,7 (4 ABBA rounds x 3 reps): copy3k **+7.7 %** (1.077x, every round 1.067 to 1.098), json-xform **-4.2 %** (0.958x, every round 0.949 to 0.969), agent-edit 1.017x (0.929 to 1.056, noise), code-gen 0.983x (noise). The criterion (clearly wins on copy3k AND json-xform without losing elsewhere) is not met: it wins on copy3k and loses on json-xform.
  The gain on Metal (+7.7 % on copy3k) is much smaller than on CUDA (1.76x to 2.06x vs MTP, +18 %): a 16-row verify pass costs more on Metal and the tokens it can add are capped by what the chain accepts. Not investigated further.

## What changed

`crates/intarsia-backend-metal/src/attn.rs` (the only engine change, about 40 lines):
- `rows_ok` accepts 2..=16 rows (new `ROWS_CHAIN_MAX = 16`); `ROWS_MAX = 8` stays the most one launch of the multi-row kernel takes (and `head_rows_exact`, the MTP head's catch-up, still uses 8). `rows_scratch` is unchanged (the scratch of one 8-row launch).
- `launch_attn_rows`, for 9..=16 rows: `row_groups(t)` splits the call into `ceil(t / 8)` groups of equal size (9 -> 5+4, 12 -> 6+6, 13 -> 7+6, 16 -> 8+8; never a 1-row group, the kernel takes 2..=8) and launches the existing multi-row kernel once per group with `pos0 + first row` and the group's rows of q and out. The encoder is `MTLDispatchTypeSerial`, so the groups reuse the one scratch slab. Each group is bitwise the 1-row split-KV decode per row (G0b), so the whole call is.
- **Why not the mcopym sketch (route 9..=16 rows to the existing per-row `AttnDecode` fallback):** that fallback is the single-pass decode kernel, which is not bitwise the split-KV decode a `--spec off` step runs on these shapes (the fallback exists for operands the split-KV kernels cannot take, where the 1-row launcher itself falls back). Row groups of the exact kernel keep the existing guarantee and cost 2 launches instead of 9..16 per full-attention layer.
- A device position (`OpCall::pos`, one value for the whole call) cannot be advanced per group, so a call with more than 8 rows and a device position (or more than 16 rows) returns an error (tested). The eager Metal verify pass uses the host position.
- Fix (2) (`ROWS_MAX` 16 inside `attn_rows_splitkv_t`) was not attempted: fix (1) exists and is small, and its extra cost is one extra launch per full-attention layer.

Also: `docs/tuning.md` (`INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY` row), `scripts/mrows16/`. Nothing in `load.rs`, `spec.rs` or the model code changed: with the verify attention available, the plan dry-run passes at `len` 15 (`raw/plan-gate.txt`; the plan has larger `scratch_bytes` for the chain, as for any `len` above 7).

## Gates

**(a) greedy ids equal `--spec off`: PASS, 10 of 10** (`scripts/mrows16/ids.sh`, `generate --chat --no-think --temperature 0 --show-ids`, `raw/ids-gate.txt`, `raw/ids/`): cells copy3k (2902 ids), json-xform (2600), code-gen (500), reason (500), chat-short (389), arms `copy=20,15` and `copy=20,12`.
Chains actually fired (and so exercised 13- and 16-row verify passes) in copy3k (180 passes at len 15, 221 at len 12) and json-xform (183 and 215; chain-token acceptance 81 % at len 15, 84 % at len 12); code-gen, reason and chat-short fired no chain in `generate` (so those three pass trivially for the changed path, as for mcopym; their served runs below do fire a few).
Served replies of all three arms of the A/B below have the same sha in every round and rep (`tables.py` prints no `IDS DIFFER`).

**(b) knob unset = main: PASS.** `plan show --json` (Metal, 35B, `--spec mtp --spec-k 2`, `--max-seq 8192`) of origin/main (`6f7691bee`, twice) and this branch: 13188 bytes, byte-identical, sha a3bb4203... (`raw/plan-gate.txt`). With the knob: `20` and `20,7` identical (sha cc3a803b...), `20,15` differs by the chain's scratch only.

**(d) tests: PASS** (`raw/tests.log`, `scripts/mrows16/tests.sh`, release, Metal): `cargo test -p intarsia-backend-metal --lib attn` (5, incl. the new `row_groups` test: every `t` 2..=16 covered contiguously in groups of 2..=8), `--test g0b_attn` (10, incl. three new: `rows_bitwise_9_to_16_rows` k = 9..=16 over the 4096-key boundary windows of the 32/8/128 shape; `rows_bitwise_9_to_16_rows_other_shapes_and_offsets` six more shapes, offset q / cache, a 16k cache; `rows_above_the_chain_cap_or_with_a_device_position_are_refused`; the registration pin now says 2..=16 accepted, 17 refused, scratch of 12 rows = 8 rows), `--test kv_once`, `--test hs_bitwise`, and `cargo test -p intarsia-cli --test docs_env_vars` (2). Every 9..=16-row row is compared bit for bit with the 1-row launch at its position.
No cpu-backend test is affected (Metal-only path).

## Gate (c): speed, `serve --max-batch 1 --max-seq 8192 --spec mtp --spec-k 2`

Method as mcopym (`scripts/mrows16/ab.sh`): one fresh server per arm and round, 4 rounds each in its own fleetq slot, the arm order reversed on even rounds (base / n20,7 / n20,15 and the reverse), 3 reps per cell, greedy streaming, per-round nonce, decode tok/s = (completion tokens - 1) / time after the first token, a warm-up request per server, cooling to thermal level 0 (at most 120 s) before every server, `notifyutil` level before / after every cell in `raw/ab-run-r*.log`.
Arms: `base` (knob unset = MTP k2), `n20L7` (`INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY=20,7`, the current default length), `n20L15` (`INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY=20,15`). Cell value = median tok/s over all reps of all rounds [min-max]; ratio = median over rounds of the round's median ratio, per-round values in brackets (round 1 to 4).

Notes:
- Thermal level (before / after each cell, 30 cells per arm): base 0 x6, 1 x8, 2 x18; n20,7 0 x12, 1 x19, 2 x1; n20,15 0 x12, 1 x18, 2 x2. Level 3+ never reached. The base arm ran hotter than the copy arms (it takes about 2 times longer per cell), which only weakens the base column; the n20,15 / n20,7 comparison ran at the same thermal levels (0 to 1 nearly always).
- Round 1 of copy3k vs base is 2.022x for n20,15 and 1.841x for n20,7 (a low base round), the other rounds are stable. copy3k and json-xform repeat in every round with the same sign: n20,15 above n20,7 on copy3k (all 4 rounds), below on json-xform (all 4 rounds). agent-edit and code-gen move by up to 6 % between rounds, which is noise on this Mac (mcopym: base alone swung 164 to 199 tok/s on code-gen).
- Cause of the json-xform loss (not separately measured, inferred from the ids-gate counters): chains stop short of their length there (acceptance of the proposed chain tokens 81 % at len 15 against 92.6 % at len 7 in mcopym), so the extra rows of the 16-row pass are verified and thrown away; copy3k accepts 99 % of 15.
- agent-edit here is 308 tokens (rep 2), so its chains are few.

## Not done / unverified

- 9..=16-row verify with a device position (refused with an error; nothing on Metal passes one to the verify pass today).
- Sampled requests, thinking mode, `--max-batch` above 1 (refused at load), tp > 1, 16k-context speed cells, real agent transcripts, `len` above 15 (the knob caps at 15), n above 20.
- Per-pass latency of 16-row passes was not measured on its own, only the end-to-end tok/s above; the per-launch cost split (2 launches of 8 rows per full-attention layer) is by construction, not profiled. A single-launch 16-row kernel (fix (2)) might shave the second launch; given the json-xform result the ceiling looks small and it was not tried.
- The `len` 12 arm was run only for the ids gate (no speed arm); the speed arms are 7 and 15.
- The A/B ran with endor's standing services up (kev, tensorfold) and another lane's llama.cpp build (`--load cpu`) ran beside the unit-test job, not beside an A/B or ids run (`--quiet gpu,cpu`).
- Commits are signed `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5` (the model that did the work), not the `Opus 5.5` line in `scan/lane-rules.md`.

## Revert

Unset `INTARSIA_SPEC_COPY` or leave `len` at its default: nothing changes (gate (b)). Reverting the PR restores the load failure at `len` above 7 on Metal; no other code changed.
