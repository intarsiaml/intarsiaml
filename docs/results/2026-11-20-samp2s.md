# samp2s: two-stream and whole-row evidence for `INTARSIA_SAMPLED_VERIFY_FAST` (2026-11-20)

Lane `samp2s`, branch `samp2s` from origin/main `edcc43c68` (includes #476 samphost and #477 ab35e), spark2 (35B NVFP4, `serve --max-batch 2 --prefix-store on`, beside the untouched production vLLM), fleetq job #10473 (owner `claude-samp2s`). Measurement and docs only: no engine code, no default changed.

## Summary

Question (decision #13): is flipping `INTARSIA_SAMPLED_VERIFY_FAST` default-on safe? #476 measured one sampled stream with `top_k` 20 only. Missing: two concurrent sampled streams, and whole-row requests (`top_k` 0 and `top_k` 100). ABBA (knob off / on / on / off), one binary (`samp2s-edcc43c687fe`), 8 reps per window per cell (16 per arm), seeded replies in every window.

| gate | result |
|---|---|
| Seeded replies identical, knob off vs on | **pass, 12 of 12 in each of the three comparisons** (a2, a3, a4 against a1; also a2 against a3). Cells: (a) `top_k` 20, two concurrent streams; (b) `top_k` 0, one stream; (c) `top_k` 100, one stream; T 0.7, top_p 0.95, 700 tokens, seeds 11 and 23, prompts 4.1k and 32.8k. Knob-off a1 and a4 are identical too, so batching timing does not move the replies. |
| Two concurrent sampled streams not worse than -2 % beyond 2 SE | **pass**: combined tok/s over the overlap window +0.6 % (2 SE 1.7 %) at 4k, +4.2 % (4.5 %) at 32k, **-2.9 % (4.1 %) at 64k**; from cold prompts at 4k -0.3 % (2.3 %). The 64k point estimate is below -2 % but inside its 2 SE (the interval is -7.0 to +1.2 %), and the cost of a verify pass is the same in all four windows (43.6 / 43.4 ms none, 43.3 / 43.5 ms fast per sequence): the tok/s differences follow the tokens per verify pass of the nonce'd prompts (see below). |
| Whole-row requests within noise | **pass, and better than neutral for `top_k` 0**: one stream, `top_k` 0, 4k: **+14.6 % (2 SE 4.4 %)**, pass cost 25.8 / 26.1 -> 22.7 / 23.4 ms (the captured draft-and-verify step now serves them, rows read back whole; fallback counter = every pass, as designed). `top_k` 100 is covered by seeded identity (12/12 with the cell's two seeds, counters show the same path) but was not timed. |
| Greedy two-stream control, no change | **pass**: -0.4 % (2 SE 3.0 %); per-pass cost 28.7 / 28.8 ms none, 28.9 / 28.6 ms fast; the knob counters stay at 0. |

**Verdict: by the lane's gates the flip is safe (yes).** Reasons, and what the evidence does not show:

1. The knob changes only one-sequence verify passes. With two sequences decoding, the batched pass (already top-k rows under the recipe's `verify_topk`) does not read it, so two-stream neutrality is by construction, and the counters show it: in the warm two-stream cell only 265 and 395 of about 10 980 verify steps per window took the knob's path (the head and tail of a request, when one stream is alone), 2.4 to 3.6 %. The tok/s of those passes is the +13 to +18 % of #476; it is diluted to a few tenths of a percent in this cell.
2. The tok/s deltas of the 2-stream cells follow tokens per verify pass, not cost per pass (table below): at 32k the fast windows' prompts accepted 2.26 / 2.31 tokens per pass against 2.19 / 2.20 (+4.2 %), at 64k 2.23 / 2.21 against 2.30 / 2.28 (-2.9 %), with equal ms per pass. The prompts carry a nonce, so each window's text differs; a seeded request cannot change its tokens (12/12 identical).
3. Not covered: `--max-batch 1` (a lone stream is then the only path; one-stream 4k / 32k / 64k was measured at `--max-batch 2` in #476, +13 to +18 %), tp 2, the 27B, `top_k` 100 timing, seeded identity of the new cells at 64k (4k and 32k here; 4.1k / 32.8k / 65.6k one-stream in #476), two sampled streams of different temperature, and `n` > 2 sequences. Revert after a flip is one variable (`INTARSIA_SAMPLED_VERIFY_FAST=0`).

## Method

- Build: `scripts/samp2s/build.sh` (samphost's, `~/intarsiaml-samp2s` on spark2; `cargo build --release --locked --features cuda -p intarsia-cli`, `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=4 MAX_JOBS=4 FLASHINFER_NVCC_THREADS=1`, 5 min, MemAvailable 71-72 GB during the build, watchdog 15 GB never near), binary stamp `samp2s-edcc43c687fe`, run under fleetq slot #10473 (`--quiet gpu,cpu --mem 55 --expect 210`, one slot for build, pilot and ABBA because the box was held by another lane's #10470 for 3 h 26 min and queueing each step separately would have waited behind other jobs again).
- Server per window (`scripts/samp2s/window.sh`): `intarsia serve --backend cuda --model <HF snapshot 1355db6a of nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4> --port 8197 --served-model-name guidance --max-seq 131072 --mem-budget 40 --profile-every 0 --max-batch 2 --prefix-store on`, bf16 KV, KV-once on (default), plus `INTARSIA_SAMPLED_VERIFY_FAST=1` in a2 / a3 only. Window order a1 none, a2 fast, a3 fast, a4 none, one fresh server each. vLLM stayed up and untouched; fleetq status during the windows (23:58) and after them showed no other job on spark2.
- Seeded (`seeded2.py`, `compare_seeded2.py`): T 0.7, top_p 0.95, 700 tokens forced (ignore_eos + min_tokens), no nonce, one fresh prompt per request (about 4.1k and 32.8k tokens). Cell (a): seed 11 and seed 23 sent together on different prompts, `top_k` 20, so the engine runs them as two sequences (starts and ends solo, the middle batched). Cells (b), (c): `top_k` 0 and `top_k` 100, one stream, seed 11 then seed 23. Compared byte for byte (text, prompt tokens, completion tokens).
- Timing (`multi.py`): N concurrent streaming requests, 512 forced tokens, per stream decode tok/s = (completion tokens - 1) / (last chunk - first chunk). **Combined over the overlap window** (all streams decoding, from the latest first chunk to the earliest last chunk; tokens counted by the share of characters that arrived inside the window) is the primary number; combined over the whole request (prefill stagger and solo tails included) is in `raw/gate2-tables.md`. Cells per window: (1) two sampled streams T 0.7, `top_k` 20, 4k / 32k / 64k x 8 reps, **warm**: each rep's two nonce'd prompts are first sent once with `max_tokens` 1, then the timed requests start together on the stored prefixes, so both streams reach decode together even at 64k (without it, the pilot showed 5-8 s of overlap at 64k, noisy per stream); (2) the same at 4k from cold prompts x 8 (organic stagger); (3) one stream T 0.7, `top_k` 0, 4k x 8; (4) two greedy streams (T 0) at 4k x 8, control. Sampled cells top_p 0.95.
- Pooled means over 16 reps per arm, 2 SE of the difference of means (`scripts/samp2s/gate2.py`). ms per verify pass and sequence = 1000 x tokens per verify (cell /metrics counters) / per-stream overlap tok/s.
- Guards: SM clock mean 2381 / 2381 / 2381 / 2382 MHz over the loaded samples (min 2307), SW power cap delta 0 us, MemAvailable minimum 36.0 GB, 0 kills, 0 warn / error lines in all four serve logs (table below); no window under 2300 MHz mean, none discarded. The window.sh guard (util > 90) saw few samples on cells this short (the GB10 utilisation field reads 0 most of the time), so the table uses samples above 25 W.

## Caveats

- 2-stream neutrality is structural (see Summary 1); the cell measures that the knob does not hurt there, and that the passes it does touch (heads and tails of requests) are not slower. A workload with many short two-stream requests will see more solo passes and so more of the #476 gain.
- Combined tok/s uses an overlap window and character-share token counting (MTP chunks carry several tokens); `raw/gate2-tables.md` also gives the whole-request combined number, which agrees within 1 point.
- Prompts carry a nonce, so each window's text differs and tokens per pass differ by up to 6 % between windows; that is the dominant noise in the 32k and 64k cells (per-pass cost agrees to 1 %).
- `top_k` 100 was identity-tested only. `top_k` 0 is better with the knob, not neutral: the captured step serves it (whole rows read back), which the lane brief did not expect.
- spark2 had no other job during the windows (fleetq status clean; #10473 was alone on the box from 23:06); MemAvailable never went below 36.0 GB.
- The slot waited 3 h 26 min behind another lane's job (#10470, "needs gpu,cpu quiet"); the first release (#10472, a `--load cpu` build) was cancelled while queued and replaced by the single slot #10473.
