# memgrow: is the MemAvailable decline of the ab35d soak bounded? (2026-11-20)

Lane `memgrow`, overnight 2026-11-19 / 20, spark1 (GB10, 121 GB unified memory), vLLM (`missioncntrl-guidance`, flight / guidance / capcom) up beside the whole time and never touched. intarsiaml at origin/main `6d2eda2e4` (binary stamp `memgrow-6d2eda2e4da8`, built on spark1 with fleetq #10331, 6 min), `serve --backend cuda --model <Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4 snapshot 1355db6a> --max-seq 131072 --mem-budget 40 --profile-every 0 --max-batch 2 --prefix-store on`, everything else default (ab35d's command line; host-tier store budget 8 GiB, no disk tier, MTP N=2, herd, SJF). Nothing in the engine was changed; no default changed.

## Summary

**Verdict: bounded on the load ab35d ran (and on the other loads tried), not leaking, and the cause is host memory of the intarsia process, not vLLM, not the page cache and not GPU memory.**

- **ab35d's -18.6 MB/min was the tail of the fill phase, not a trend.** Its own series, refit: -18.6 +- 10.3 MB/min when the fit starts at sample 5, +1.5 +- 10.9 when it starts at sample 8, +21 +- 16 from sample 12; the first 5 samples alone moved MemAvailable by 11 GB (host store and workspaces filling). 0.47 GB over 25 minutes against an 8 GiB store budget could never have been the store still filling anyway: the store was full in minute 3 (`host.bytes` 8.58 GB of 8.59 GB).
- **Run A, the same load generator for 105 minutes (2,646 requests, 0 errors), sampled every 30 s: the server's footprint is 40.4 GB, reached in 10 to 20 minutes, then flat.** MemAvailable 82.0 GB before the server, 53.1 GB with it loaded and idle, **41.6 GB from minute 20 to minute 106** (sd 175 MB, min 40.9, max 41.9). Slope from minute 30: **-15 +- 44 MB/h** (from minute 10: -60 +- 31 MB/h, still settling). With the server stopped MemAvailable returned to 82.3 GB (+0.24 GB against before; 0 processes of ours left).
- **Components (box-wide counters, closing to 0.2 GB):** the process's GPU memory 27.25 GB (nvidia-smi per process, flat to +-130 MB for 105 minutes; weights 21.4 GB + 5.4 GB KV pool + workspaces) plus its anonymous host memory 12.85 GB (`RssAnon`: the 8.4 GB host store tier plus 4.4 GB of other heap) plus 0.2 GB of kernel slab, page tables and shared memory. **Page cache did not move (Cached +47 MB)**: the store has no disk tier by default (budget 0), so the "page cache from store disk writes" explanation is excluded by configuration. vLLM did not move at all (GPU 26,768 MB, cgroup 8,253 MB, RSS 3,312 MB, identical in every sample of every run).
- **Other request mixes move the process's host memory by a few GB but did not grow without bound in the 30 to 60 minutes tried.** Mixed traffic (run B: sizes 0.5k to 93k tokens, aborts, tools, thinking, 996 requests, 60 min): non-host anon crept from 2.5 to 3.1 GB, **+0.5 GB/h (2.5 to 4 SE) and not saturated when the run ended**: the one unresolved signal. Many short requests (runs C and D, 3,500 and 3,050 requests): non-host anon swung between 1.9 and 6.6 GB (a burst of +2.7 GB in 3 minutes and a drop of 1.3 GB in C; flat in D). One `malloc_trim(0)` at the end of D handed 1.1 GB back, so a good part of the swings is free memory glibc keeps (arena tops, holes), not live data.
- **Operational reading for a cutover:** at the plateau the server holds 40.4 GB; with vLLM resident the lowest MemAvailable seen in any of the five runs was 38.8 GB (kill line 15 GB, earlyoom at 2 %), so there are about 24 GB of margin. A leak below roughly 0.1 GB/h (A) or 0.5 GB/h (B's creep) is not excluded by runs of this length; a days-long run on production-like traffic with the sampler of this lane (`scripts/memgrow/sampler.sh`) would settle it, and the mitigation below is cheap if B's creep turns out real.

## 1. What the ab35d data say (reasoning from the existing raw files)

`docs/results/2026-11-19-ab35d-cutover-scoreboard/raw/sample-w4.tsv` (branch `ab35d`; 26 samples, 60 s apart, 22:20:45 to 22:45:48):

| sample | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 to 5 | 6 to 25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MemAvailable MB | 48,641 | 41,386 | 39,249 | 37,948 to 37,330 | 36,801 to 36,101 (36.5 GB mean, sd about 0.3) |
| store host tier MB | 0 | 6,394 | 8,590 | 8,272 to 8,583 | 7,812 to 8,589 |

- **18.6 MB/min times 25 min is 0.47 GB, 6 % of the 8 GiB store budget.** The store reached its budget in minute 2 to 3 (`intarsia_prefix_store_tier_bytes{tier="host"}` 8.59 GB of 8.59 GB budget, host evictions started in sample 2), so it cannot still be growing toward the budget, and a disk tier was off (`tier_bytes{tier="disk"}` 0, budget 0).
- **The reported slope depends on where the fit starts:** from sample 5 (the "store full" point of the doc) -18.6 +- 10.3 MB/min (1.8 SE); from sample 8 +1.5 +- 10.9; from sample 12 +21 +- 16. The first five samples contain the fill (48.6 to 37.3 GB). The residual wobble (+-0.3 GB) matches the store tier's own sawtooth (7.8 to 8.6 GB).
- **Its `rss_mb` column (162 MB, constant) never measured the engine**: `serve` runs a supervisor and the engine as its child; `ps -o rss -p <supervisor>` is the supervisor's 162 MB. Neither does RSS cover the GPU allocations on a GB10 (unified memory, cudaMalloc is not in /proc). This lane samples the child and reads the GPU side from nvidia-smi per process.

So the data could not say more than "flat after the fill, within noise". The longer runs below test that.

## 2. Method

**Box and rules.** spark1 only; vLLM serving flight / guidance / capcom stays up (nothing `--takes-down`); fleetq slot for every job (build #10331 `--load cpu --mem 55`; runs `--mem 52`); watchdog on the box (`wd.sh`, every 20 s, kills the supervisor and its children by pid below 15 GB MemAvailable; 0 kills, lowest MemAvailable 38.8 GB); earlyoom active; `df` 92 %, the box dir `~/intarsiaml-memgrow` (build, out) was removed at the end.

**Sampler** (`scripts/memgrow/sampler.sh`, runs on the box, 30 s): a keepalive-bound loop that survives the server, so each run has a **base** phase (vLLM only, 90 s), **start** (server loading), **settle** (server up, idle, 2 min), **load**, **idle** (the load stopped, server up), **stop** and **after** (server gone, 2.5 min). Per row: `/proc/meminfo` (MemFree, MemAvailable, Buffers, Cached, Active/Inactive anon and file, Unevictable, Mlocked, AnonPages, Mapped, Shmem, Dirty, Writeback, Slab, SReclaimable, SUnreclaim, KernelStack, PageTables, Percpu, VmallocUsed, AnonHugePages, SwapFree); the engine's `/proc/<pid>/status` (VmRSS, RssAnon, RssFile, RssShmem, VmSwap, VmData, Threads) and `smaps_rollup` (Pss, Pss_Anon, Pss_File, Pss_Shmem, Private_Dirty, Locked, AnonHugePages), where `<pid>` is the supervisor's child, re-resolved every tick (the ipid column); the vLLM container's cgroup (`memory.current`, anon, file, shmem, ...) and RSS of its main and EngineCore processes; **per-process GPU memory from `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps` (works on this GB10: engine and vLLM EngineCore each have a number; the box-level `memory.used` is N/A)**; and the server's `/metrics`: requests, kv bytes used, device memory free, store entries, inserts, bytes inserted and restored, host / device / disk tier bytes, objects, evictions. Raw: `docs/results/2026-11-20-memgrow/raw/mem-<run>.csv` (77 columns), phase marks `marks-<run>.tsv`, watchdog log `wd-<run>.log`, server log `serve-<run>.log` (A to C) / `serve-D.log`.

**Loads** (`scripts/memgrow/soak.sh`, `soak-mixed.sh`, `soak-d.sh`; the generators run on endor against `spark-4e77:8191`, as in ab35d):

Request mixes are a synthetic stand-in: nothing here is real missioncntrl traffic. `mixed.py` was checked against a local fake server first (125 requests, 0 failures after fixing a socket-close bug in the abort path).

**Attribution tools.** (1) Box-wide counters before / during / after the server. (2) nvidia-smi per process for GPU memory, `RssAnon` for host memory. (3) `scripts/memgrow/smaps_cat.py`: the engine's resident anonymous memory split into `[heap]`, 64 MiB-aligned `rw-p` mappings (glibc thread-arena heaps), other anonymous mappings of 1 MiB and more, and the rest. (4) `malloc_trim(0)` in the live process. **Not used: ptrace / gdb (yama `ptrace_scope` 1 forbids attaching to a process we did not start as its parent; no sudo), heaptrack, jemalloc (the engine uses glibc malloc; no custom allocator in the tree).** `malloc_stats()` turned out unusable here: it prints 32-bit-wrapped "in use" numbers (arena 5 showed "in use 3.98 GB, system 0.53 GB" while the host store alone is 8.5 GB in that arena); `libmemstat.c` now prints `malloc_info()` (64-bit XML) instead, which only run E has.

## 4. Runs B, C, D: other traffic

@@BCD-TABLES@@

What each shows (`raw/analysis-B.txt`, `-C.txt`, `-D.txt`):

- **B (mixed, aborts, long prompts):** MemAvailable 43.9 to 42.9 GB, engine GPU flat at 27.1 to 27.4 GB (steps of up to +0.4 GB that came back), RssAnon 10.4 to 11.4 GB. Non-host anon (RssAnon minus the store tier) 2.46, 2.66, 2.83, 2.86, 3.11, 3.14 GB per 10 minutes: **+0.55 +- 0.24 GB/h from minute 30 (store sawtooth regressed out; +0.49 +- 0.17 from minute 20, +0.59 +- 0.11 from minute 10)**; MemAvailable -0.2 to -0.5 GB/h in the same windows. The run ended (60 min) before it could show a plateau; A's non-host anon plateaued at 4.4 GB, so B may simply be filling a larger or slower pool, but this lane cannot say. `raw/mixed-B/mixed-7.jsonl` has every request (kind, prompt, completion, status); no single request kind lines up with the steps.
- **C (many short requests):** 9.9 GB of RssAnon in minutes 0 to 10, then a **ramp of +2.7 GB in 3 minutes at minute 14 (10.8 to 13.3 GB), +1.8 GB more by minute 19 (peak 15.1 GB), a drop of 1.3 GB at minute 21, and a regrow to 14.6 GB**, ending at 13.8 GB. The engine's own GPU memory did not move and nothing in the request mix changed at that point. In the next 20 s of an smaps diff taken while it ramped, 4 new 64 MiB-aligned arena heaps filled (289 MB net): glibc thread-arena heaps.
- **D (same as C):** no burst this time: RssAnon 9.9, 10.9, 11.1 GB, flat to the MB from minute 19 to the trim (11,113 MB in every sample), then one `malloc_trim(0)` at the end of the load: **RssAnon 11,113 to 9,992 MB, 1.12 GB handed back in one step**, no regrowth during the idle phase. smaps split just before: `[heap]` 0.16 GB, **64 MiB-aligned arena heaps 9.1 to 10.2 GB in 157 to 174 mappings (the store tier lives there)**, other anonymous 1.5 GB falling to 0.7 GB (large chunks moving from direct mmap into arenas as glibc's dynamic mmap threshold rises).

So the picture under short-request traffic is a glibc arena sawtooth: the store's host objects (a few sizes, 1 to 30 MB) are `malloc`ed and freed constantly, and glibc keeps freed pages in arenas (idle thread arenas hold up to 40 MB each; holes inside the big arena stay resident) until a trim. The size of the sawtooth differs from run to run (non-host anon peak 6.6 GB in C, 2.6 GB in D) with the same request sequence and different timing.

## 5. Attribution

| candidate | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| page cache from store disk writes | **excluded** | no disk tier (budget 0, `tier_bytes{tier="disk"}` 0); Cached +46 MB over 105 min; MemAvailable counts clean cache anyway |
| host tier growing toward its budget | **excluded** as a continuing cause | full in minute 1 to 3 in every run; mean 8.4 GB, sawtooth 7.8 to 8.59 GB, slope 0 +- 50 MB/h |
| vLLM or other processes | **excluded** | vLLM GPU 26,768 MB, cgroup 8,253 MB, RSS 3,312 MB constant in all samples of all five runs; box before / after the server within 0.25 GB |
| GPU memory of the engine (cudaMalloc, KV pool, workspaces) | **excluded** | nvidia-smi per process 27.1 to 27.5 GB, slope -6 +- 41 MB/h from minute 30 in A |
| anonymous host memory of the engine (`RssAnon`) | **the whole decline, and bounded under the shipping load** | A: plateau 12.85 GB from minute 20, +17 +- 44 MB/h from minute 30; B: +0.5 GB/h not saturated (unresolved); C / D: arena sawtooth, 1.1 GB reclaimable by `malloc_trim(0)` |
| a leak (live data growing) | **not shown, not excluded for small rates** | flat for 90 minutes in A, returned to the OS at exit; B's creep unresolved; no heap profiler (malloc_info of E, below, is the only live-vs-free split) |

## 6. Verdict

**Bounded.** On the ab35d / soak2 load (2 streams, 8k to 33k-token multi-turn, store on, defaults), the server's total footprint beside vLLM is **40.4 GB (27.25 GB GPU + 12.85 GB anon + 0.2 GB kernel), reached after 10 to 20 minutes, with drift -15 +- 44 MB/h (A, from minute 30), i.e. no component grows at more than about 0.1 GB/h at 2 SE over the 76 minutes measured at the plateau**. The component that moves at all is the engine's anonymous host memory (store host tier 8.4 GB plus 4.4 GB of other heap), not vLLM, not the page cache, not GPU memory. ab35d's -18.6 MB/min is the end of the fill, refit to +1.5 +- 10.9 MB/min from its sample 8.

**Not closed:** (1) run B's +0.5 GB/h creep under mixed traffic did not saturate in 60 minutes; the hypothesis (glibc arena retention, same family as C and D) is supported by D's `malloc_trim` (1.1 GB freed) but not proven: the live-vs-free split by `malloc_info` exists only for run E's load. (2) Real production traffic (thinking on, tools, client disconnects at random, more than 2 streams) was not available; the five runs are 4.5 hours of synthetic load in all. (3) Days: nothing here covers a restart, a model swap or days of uptime. (4) Thread count differed between runs (27 to 32 in A, 7 to 13 in B to D) with the same binary and flags; not investigated.

**What to do for the cutover:** run the sampler (`scripts/memgrow/sampler.sh`, 30 s, no root needed) beside the first production shadow day, alert on MemAvailable below 20 GB (kill line 15 GB, earlyoom 2 %), and expect 38.8 to 42 GB of MemAvailable at steady state with vLLM resident. @@E-OPS@@
