  kernels already carry a threadgroup swizzle (`swz_log`: `2^swz_log` consecutive threadgroups along M share an N tile).
  P1 spike (2026-10-14, `docs/results/raw/2026-10-14-metal-prefill-p1/bench-*.txt`) measured down M = 256 / 1024 / 2048 at
  51 / 53 / 48 TFLOPS (64x64w4) and swizzle 0..3 neutral; the roadmap's 35 comes from mac-plan's run of 2026-11-15
