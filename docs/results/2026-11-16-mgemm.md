# Lane mgemm: NAX prefill GEMM on long-K / small-N shapes (Mac roadmap idea #7, 2026-11-16)

Status: **findings only, nothing wired in.** On a quiet box the shipped NAX GEMM already runs the roadmap's down cell
(M = 2048) at 47 to 52 TFLOPS: the premise (36 vs 62) did not reproduce. Neither tile form tried beats the shipped kernel
beyond run-to-run noise. Form (b), a sequential K-split, turned out **bitwise** the shipped kernel (192 of 192 checks),
which is worth knowing, but it is not faster; it lives on side branch `mgemm-ks` (`3b8e4882e`), not in this PR. Engine
sources in this PR are identical to the base; the PR carries this doc, raw data, scripts, the 27B / 35B bench shapes in
`examples/gemm_nax.rs`, and a LOG row.

## 1. Verdict

* **Premise not reproduced on a quiet box.** The roadmap's "down at 36 TFLOPS against 62 for square shapes" comes from
  mac-plan's run of 2026-11-15, whose box state is unknown. Today, with the GPU at 0 % before and during the run (#9727,
  #9729), down M = 2048 on the shipped pick runs 47.0 / 52.1 TFLOPS, gu 54 to 57, qo 62 to 64; P1 (2026-10-14) had
  48 to 55 for down. In #9725, whose idle sample read 23 % GPU busy, the same cell read 41.1. Run-to-run spread on one
  cell is about +-5 TFLOPS (compare #9727 pool 0 and pool 3 GiB, or 32x128w2 at 53.0 in one run and 42.5 in the next).
* **Stop rule.** The shipped kernel itself reaches 48+ on the down shape at M = 2048 on a quiet box (52.1 in #9729), and
  neither form beats it within the same interleaved run. There is no winner to wire through `pick_variant`, so tasks 3
  and 4 do not apply.
* **The 27B down shape (d27, 5120 x 17408)** runs 46.3 (M = 2048) against 49.6 for its own square control (sq27,
  5120 x 5120) in the same run: a gap of about 7 %, inside the noise. Its cause is not established. A cache-footprint
  model fit #9725 well (qo at M = 2048, A + W = 48 MB, 62; qo at M = 4096 or sq27, over 64 MB, 46 to 50), but the K-split
  at 6 passes cuts d27's per-pass A + W slab to about 41 MB and the rate stayed at about 46, so either the f32 partial
  traffic cancels the cache gain or footprint is not the cause. I do not claim a mechanism.
* **35B shared expert.** As a GEMM alone it is not slow: se_gu (N 512, K 2048) 47.7 on the shipped pick and 50 to 51 on
  the best tiles at M = 2048, se_dn (N 2048, K 512) 51.6 (#9725). At M = 512 it is 31 to 32 (a 0.5 GFLOP call, launch
  bound). So the roadmap's "about 14 TFLOPS effective in prefill" is not the NAX GEMM. My inference, not measured: the
  NVFP4 path's per-call weight decode to bf16 scratch, its extra dispatches and launch overhead on a 1 MB weight, i.e.
  idea #6's scope. The engine path was not profiled here.

**Form (b) exactness.** `KSBITS` in `raw/ks1.txt`: 192 checks (qo, down, d27, sq27 x M 512 / 2048 / 4096 x 4 tiles x 1, 2,

one f32 accumulator (lane mnr), so storing the f32 partial and resuming with `multiply_accumulate` is exact. A
sequential K-split is therefore a bitwise tool for later work (for example a decode-weight pass per K range, idea #6),
even though it does not pay here.
