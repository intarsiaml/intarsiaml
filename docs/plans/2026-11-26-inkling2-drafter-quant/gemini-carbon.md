**Verdict — Custom Drafter:** **Do not train or fine-tune a custom drafter; build the zero-training speculation levers `main` leaves off.** Inkling-2-Mini already ships a purpose-trained DFlash block drafter, `DSpark` (`mtp.safetensors`: 3 layers, 4 target taps at visits 5/23/41/48, rank-256 Markov head; [dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L5-L20)), which costs ~2.1–3.0 ms/cycle (~6–8% of a cycle) on R9700 ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L102-L103)) and reaches 72–82% acceptance on 1k–16k greedy continuations ([2026-11-26-inkling2-spec-cut.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-spec-cut.md#L91-L96)). Self-distilling DSpark cannot run inside `tatooine`'s `<=5 min` windows or `endor`'s 140 W budget (requiring a separate DGX Spark ask) and yields at most **+6–8% modelled** greedy tok/s. Meanwhile, `main` disables DSpark entirely whenever `temperature > 0`—including `serve`'s `temperature = 1.0` default ([recipes/inkling2.toml](recipes/inkling2.toml#L40-L41), [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L185-L187))—and omits `propose_copy` prompt-lookup on `SpecMode::Dflash` ([spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L87-L90), [spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L689-L713)). Fixing those two levers delivers **1.15–1.45x modelled** on real sampled traffic and multi-token leaps on code/JSON edits with zero training.

**Verdict — Custom IntarsiaML Quant:** **Yes, in two tiers—default-on lossless P12 on HIP first, then opt-in `fp8` (and optionally GPTQ-calibrated mixed `NVFP4+fp8`) dense quant—without inventing a custom container format.** On R9700, dense `in_proj`, `wo`, and `unembed` still stream in bf16 (`3.50 GB` of the `5.49 GB/tok` floor, taking `5.64 ms` of a `13.01 ms` step; [2026-11-25-inkling2-hip.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-hip.md#L55), [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md#L44-L50)). Porting lossless P12 (`-0.88 GB/tok`, already default-on for Metal in [p12.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/quant/p12.rs#L353-L361)) is bitwise-identical to HIP's existing SIMT `in_proj_gemv_kernel` ([ink_proj.hip](kernels/hip/ink_proj.hip#L6-L7)), default-eligible, and buys **+10–11% modelled** plain decode (`~85.5 tok/s`) and **+5–6% modelled** spec decode (`~121 tok/s` at 4k). For opt-in lossy speed + VRAM headroom, porting G3-passing `fp8_e4m3` dense+head (`3.75 GB/tok`; [2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L203-L208)) to HIP and tuning Metal's generic `MatmulFp8` GEMV ([2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L249-L252)) reaches **~97 tok/s plain on R9700 (+25% modelled)** and **~92 tok/s plain on Metal (+16% modelled over P12+screen)** while freeing `1.74 GB` VRAM on the 32 GB R9700.

### 1. Custom Drafter vs. Speculation Levers

**Verify-cost arithmetic on R9700.** Measured HIP times ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L99-L103)): 1-row decode = `13.30 ms` **(measured)** (`77.4 tok/s` short sweep); graphed verify `V(2)=16.48 ms`, `V(3)=19.00 ms`, `V(4)=20.80 ms` **(measured)**; draft block (`2.11 ms`) + head/Markov/ingest (`~0.9–1.6 ms`) = `~3.0 ms` **(measured)** (~8% of a cycle; verify is >90%). At `k=3` (`R=4`), one full cycle is `20.80 + 3.0 = 23.8 ms` **(modelled)**; with `2.46 tok/pass` **(measured)** on the 16-prompt `cut 0.5` set ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L158)), `2.46 / 0.0238 = 103.4 tok/s` **(modelled)** vs. `103.5 tok/s` **(measured)**.

**Why fine-tuning/training DSpark has low ROI.** Across 2,564 greedy continuations, DSpark already hits `72.2% / 72.2% / 73.7%` conditional step acceptance **(measured)** (`93.3%` code, `79%` reasoning, `52–64%` short chat/story; [analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L12-L16)). W4A4-to-W4A16 off-path drift affects just `83 / 2647` (`3.1%`) anchors ([analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L228-L232)). Even if fine-tuning raised uniform step acceptance `α` by `+4–5 pp` (`0.72 -> 0.76–0.77`), `E[tok/pass] = 1 + α + α² + α³` moves from `2.61` to `2.78–2.82` (**+6.5–8.0% modelled**, `103.5 -> ~110–112 tok/s` short).
- **If training were requested anyway (Explicit Separate Ask: `spark1` + `spark2`, 2x GB10 121 GB):** Keep the exact `DSpark` architecture (`0.636 GB` bf16, frozen target `unembed`) so zero runtime kernels change; `intarsia mtp-dump` only supports Qwen `[mtp]` heads ([mtp.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/mtp.rs#L630-L645)), and 1-layer LoRA MTP heads reached only `1.44 tok/verify` ([2026-11-16-mdraft.md](docs/results/2026-11-16-mdraft.md#L33-L45)). Neither `tatooine` (`<=5 min` windows) nor `endor` (140 W adapter clamping sustained GPU to ~43 W; [2026-11-25-inkling2-mode.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-mode.md#L108-L110)) can generate ~25M rollout tokens (~70 GPU-hours at 100 tok/s). Do not write 4-tap hidden states to disk (`18.4 KB/tok` = `460 GB` per 25M tokens); run online W4A16 teacher rollouts (70% greedy/low-temp self-distillation, 30% chat/story) into a PyTorch DSpark trainer on 2x GB10 (~14–20 hours **modelled**).

**Cheaper spec levers ranked against training:**
1. **Sampled DSpark (`t > 0`):** Today `t > 0` runs plain decode (`0` verify passes; [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L185-L187)) even though `serve` defaults to `temperature = 1.0` ([2026-11-21-inkling2-hostgap.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-hostgap.md#L376-L380)). `SampledSpec::Match` is already implemented and distribution-exact ([spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L168-L177), [spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L1932-L1947)), and softcapped verify logits are already fixed ([ink_spec.hip](kernels/hip/ink_spec.hip#L44-L55)). Wiring `Match` (or top-64 Markov rejection) unlocks **+15–45% modelled** tok/s on real `serve` requests.
2. **Copy / prompt-lookup (`propose_copy`) + DSpark fallback:** Already built with `CopyBackoff` for Qwen MTP ([spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L390-L418), [spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L562-L569)). Trying `propose_copy` before `dspark_draft` (ingesting accepted copy taps via `dspark_ingest`, which supports up to 512 rows; [dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L602-L615)) gives `4–8 tok/pass` **(modelled)** on code edits/JSON/RAG at ~0 prose cost.
3. **HIP draft-length sweep (`cut 0.5`, `k=4, 5`):** Untested on HIP ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L135-L137)); Metal saw `+1.5%` at 1k/16k and `-1.2%` at short/4k ([2026-11-26-inkling2-spec-cut.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-spec-cut.md#L71-L76)). Note `R >= 5` bumps `ink_moe.hip` from LDS class 0 (`<=32` pairs) to class 1 (`<=128` pairs; [ink_moe.hip](kernels/hip/ink_moe.hip#L66-L70)): worth a single 3-min probe (**0 to +4% modelled** at 4k/16k).
4. **Cheaper verify/row & tree verify:** Screened verify heads and draft row-table attention were already measured at `0%` on Metal ([2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md#L171-L190)); tree verify bloats MoE expert unions (`18.9 -> 27.6` experts/visit for `R=4 -> 8`; [analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L4-L7)) and breaks linear `InkConv`/`InkKv` rings ([inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L393-L410)). Cut verify ms via **P12 / `fp8` target weights** instead.

**Why HIP P12 is unusually clean:** On CUDA, `in_proj` used `mma` so switching to P12 GEMV changed reduction order and nudged G2 short decode KL over `4.0e-3` ([2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md#L1089)). HIP's `in_proj_gemv_kernel` is *already* the SIMT GEMV twin of `inkling2_p12.cu` ([ink_proj.hip](kernels/hip/ink_proj.hip#L6-L7)), so HIP P12 preserves the exact per-lane/butterfly reduction order—100% bitwise for `R=1..16`. Replacing GPU bf16 `unembed` (`<=16` rows everywhere; [2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L174-L175)) keeps R9700 VRAM delta to `+0.97 GB` (~21.5 GB of 32 GB).

**Why mixed `NVFP4+fp8` needs 3-pass GPTQ:** Plain RTN `mxfp4` (`per-32 e8m0`) on dense attention failed G3 (`KL21 = 2.32e-2 / 3.75e-2`, 4–8 confident misses; [2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L204-L226)) because `wr_du` (`[384, 2304]`, just `42.5 MB` total) and `qk_l2_clip` (`22.627`) collapse under coarse scales. Keeping `wr_du`/sinks/unembed in `fp8` and fitting GPTQ Hessians `H = ∑_{p=0..2} X_pᵀ X_p` pooled across all 3 core-loop passes ([2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L32)) takes ~15 min on `endor` CPU over 512 calibration prompts (no Sparks required).

**On-disk checkpoint format:** Do **not** invent a proprietary binary container for P12 or RTN `fp8` (P12 packs in `0.7 s` at load; [2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L129-L131)). Do build: (a) a content-addressed cache file for DSpark's Markov `fold_w` + `mxfp4` draft head (`2.1 s` on `tatooine`, `5.9 s` on `endor`; [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L67), [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L1110)) to save scarce `<=5 min` window time, and (b) standard `safetensors` export (`intarsia quantize`) for GPTQ-calibrated quants via `QuantScheme` ([scheme.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/quant/scheme.rs#L1-L35)) and `reference_kind = format` goldens ([accuracy.md](docs/accuracy.md#L278-L303)), optionally pre-deinterleaving `w13` (`10.4 GB`, currently heap-repacked on every load unlike zero-copy `w2`; [inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L464-L475), [inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L711-L772)).

### 3. Interaction & Ordering

- **Lossless P12 target × DSpark:** Zero tap or logit change (`69.6%` acceptance, `478` passes identical on Metal; [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L1070-L1074)). Wire HIP P12 up to `R=16` on `in_proj`, `wo`, and `verify_head` (matching CUDA brief 26; [2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md#L1107-L1115)) so `R=2..4` verify passes also save `-1.25 ms/pass`.
- **Lossy target × DSpark:** `fp8` shifts target logits by `KL ~ 3–5e-3` ([2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L207))—one-third of the W4A4-vs-W4A16 gap DSpark already absorbs—so acceptance shifts `< 1–2 pp` **(modelled)**. Because dense quant cuts fixed pass time while per-row MoE union cost (`+2.5 ms/row`) stays flat, the relative row cost `r` rises (`0.19 -> 0.24`), keeping `k=3, cut 0.5` optimal. **Bug to fix before pre-quantized checkpoints:** `dspark::load` rejects non-bf16 `unembed.weight` ([dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L390-L397)); teach it to dequantize `unembed` for `fold()`.
- **Quantizing DSpark itself:** **Leave as-is.** DSpark's base head is *already* `mxfp4` (`0.24 GB`; [dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L409-L425)), and `INTARSIA_DSPARK_LAYERS=fp8` ([dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L321-L337)) already measured `-3.1%` at 1k due to 8-row/ingest overhead ([2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md#L1027-L1035)).
- **Order:** (1) Lossless HIP P12 -> (2) Sampled + Copy speculation -> (3) Opt-in `fp8` HIP + fast Metal `MatmulFp8` -> (4) Optional GPTQ `NVFP4+fp8` `safetensors` -> (5) Never fine-tune a drafter before freezing target quant.

### 4. Plan

**Top 5 Ranked Items**
1. **Lossless P12 (`in_proj`, `wo`, `unembed`, `R=1..16`) on HIP (default-on candidate)** — *Effort:* 8–10 agent-h, 4× `<=4 min` `tatooine` windows. *Gate:* `p12` unit + rows-exact `R=1..16` bitwise == bf16; `inkling2` (17) + `inkling2_dspark` (4); greedy md5 (`g64/g256/1k/4k`) P12 on == off == spec on; paired ABBA >= +2%. *Gain:* **+10–11% plain (`~85.5 tok/s`)**, **+5–6% 4k spec (`~121 tok/s`) modelled** on R9700.
2. **Exact sampled speculation (`SampledSpec::Match` + softcapped verify) for DSpark on HIP & Metal** — *Effort:* 6–8 agent-h, 2× `<=4 min` `tatooine` + 2× `endor` (`45 s`-gap within-process) windows. *Gate:* `inkling2_verify_softcap`; multinomial chi-square law check (`p > 0.01` at `t=0.6, 1.0`, matching [spec.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/spec.rs#L1932-L1947)); `t=0` md5 unchanged; paired `serve` A/B. *Gain:* **+15–45% modelled** on `t > 0` `serve` traffic (vs. `1.00x` today).
3. **`propose_copy` prompt-lookup ahead of DSpark (`SpecMode::Dflash`)** — *Effort:* 4–5 agent-h, 1× `tatooine` + 1× `endor` window. *Gate:* greedy md5 (`copy+dspark` == `--spec off`) on short/1k/4k/16k + code-edit prompts; tap/KV state test across multi-token copy accepts; prose regression <= 1%. *Gain:* **+25–60% modelled** on code/JSON/rewrite prompts, ~0% prose cost.
4. **Opt-in `fp8_e4m3` dense + `unembed` on HIP, plus tuned `MatmulFp8` `wo`/head GEMV on Metal** — *Effort:* 10–12 agent-h, 4× `tatooine` + 2× `endor` windows. *Gate:* **G3** short/long/4k (`KL21 <= 1.05e-2 / 1.65e-2 / 1.4e-2`, misses `<= 1 / 2 / 9`; [2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L335-L343)); `fp8` spec-on md5 == `fp8` spec-off md5. *Gain:* **~97 tok/s plain (+25%) + 1.74 GB VRAM on R9700**; **~92 tok/s plain (+16%) on Metal modelled**.
5. **DSpark fold cache + opt-in 3-pass-GPTQ mixed `NVFP4+fp8` dense `safetensors` (`intarsia quantize`)** — *Effort:* 14–18 agent-h (including `dspark.rs:391` fix), 2× `endor` CPU/GPU slots + 3× `tatooine` windows. *Gate:* cached load bitwise == cold load; `reference_kind = format` golden ([accuracy.md](docs/accuracy.md#L278-L303)) passing **G3 short/long/4k**. *Gain:* **`-2.1 s` / `-5.9 s` load time**; **~107 tok/s R9700 (+38%) / ~96 tok/s Metal (+20%) plain modelled**.

**Don't Do List**
- **Don't train/fine-tune a drafter on `tatooine` or `endor`** — violates `<=5 min` / 140 W budgets for at most +6–8% modelled over shipped DSpark ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L102-L103)).
- **Don't use RTN `mxfp4` (`e8m0`) on dense attention or `unembed`** — already tried and failed G3 (`2.32e-2 / 3.75e-2`, 4–8 confident misses; [2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L204-L226)).
- **Don't re-quantize layers 0–19 NVFP4 experts to 3–4 bpw** — no public bf16 master exists ([2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L92-L98)); double-quantizing 4-bit weights destroys quality.
- **Don't build tree verify for Inkling-2** — MoE expert unions balloon (`18.9 -> 27.6` experts/visit; [analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L4-L7)) and `InkConv`/`InkKv` rings are linear-only.
- **Don't screen multi-row verify heads (`R >= 2`) or add draft row-table attention** — already measured at `0%` (`0.996–1.004x`) and noise ([2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md#L171-L190)).
- **Don't quantize DSpark layers to 4-bit or flip `INTARSIA_DSPARK_LAYERS=fp8` default** — `fp8` draft layers regressed `-3.1%` at 1k ([2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md#L1027-L1035)).
- **Don't quantize KV cache to `fp8` chasing 4k tok/s** — 40/48 SWA-1024 visits cap 4k KV at `0.30 GB` (`<2%` speed ceiling; [2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L53-L58)).
