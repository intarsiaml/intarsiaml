### Verdicts

**Custom drafter: Don't train one from scratch—and don't fine-tune DSpark until cheaper spec-path levers and target quantization land.** The shipped DSpark drafter (`mtp.safetensors`, 636 MB, 3 layers, 4 target taps, parallel 8-row block + rank-256 Markov head; [2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L266-L278)) already hits 72.2% position-1 and 72–82% conditional greedy acceptance ([analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L12-L16)) at ~2.1 ms block cost on R9700 ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L102-L103)). Training a competitive replacement is infeasible inside tatooine's ≤5 min windows or endor's 140 W thermal budget and would require the Sparks. Meanwhile, zero-training spec levers—sweeping `kmax=4..7` on HIP's flat verify curve, wiring DSpark into exact sampled speculation (`engine/keyed.rs`) for real `T>0` chat traffic, and lowering verify cost via dense weight packing/quantization—deliver larger, faster, bitwise/distribution-exact gains. Fine-tuning DSpark via self-distilled KL on the Sparks is a distant conditional step only if a lossy quantized target erodes acceptance or a narrow production domain justifies it.

**Custom IntarsiaML quant: Yes—in two tiers (lossless P12 on HIP as default, then opt-in FP8 and calibrated mixed NVFP4+FP8 dense+unembed).** Experts are already NVFP4 (1.58 GB/tok) plus 4 coda MXFP8 layers (0.26 GB/tok), so **64% of the 5.49 GB/tok decode floor sits in bf16 attention projections (2.58 GB) and unembed (0.92 GB)** ([2026-11-25-inkling2-hip.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-hip.md#L149-L151)). On HIP, porting lossless **P12** (already default-on on Metal and CUDA; [p12.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/quant/p12.rs#L350-L361)) is a clean default-eligible +10–12% plain / +5–7% spec win. For opt-in speed, **FP8 E4M3** dense+unembed with per-subtensor/per-row scales (fixing the current shared-scale bottleneck in [inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L588-L610)) saves 1.74 GB/tok and passes G3 uncalibrated; **calibrated mixed NVFP4+FP8** (NVFP4 on bulk projections+unembed, FP8 on sensitive `wr_du`/`sink`/KL-flagged layers, saved as pre-packed safetensors) saves 2.46 GB/tok (-45% floor) using existing NVFP4/FP8 kernels without repeating the killed MXFP4 RTN failure ([2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L194-L195)).

### 1. Custom Drafter vs. Cheaper Spec-Path Levers

**Why stock DSpark is hard to beat by training.** DSpark was trained by Thinking Machines directly on Inkling-2-Mini's 4 internal taps (visits 5, 23, 41, 48). Its greedy acceptance on `code` is 93.3% (4.18/7 accepted), `chat_reasoning` 78–80% (2.65/7), and overall greedy golden 72.2% P1 ([analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L15-L16), [L444-L458](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L444-L458)). The W4A4→W4A16 distribution shift accounts for only ~1.0 pp of P1 acceptance (0.722 vs 0.712 including off-path anchors; [analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L228-L232)). An autoregressive EAGLE-3/small-LM drafter would run $k$ serial passes instead of DSpark's single 8-row parallel block, increasing draft latency.

**Arithmetic from the measured R9700 verify table** ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L99-L103), [L147-L149](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L147-L149)):
- Measured HIP verify times (graph on, 1k): $T_v(1)=13.30$, $T_v(2)=16.48$ (+3.18), $T_v(3)=19.00$ (+2.52), $T_v(4)=20.80\text{ ms}$ (+1.80 ms). Extrapolating along the measured expert-union curve ($U(1..6)=8.0, 12.5, 16.0, 18.9, 21.4, 23.7$; [analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L4-L6)) gives modelled $T_v(5)\approx 22.35$, $T_v(6)\approx 23.75\text{ ms}$.
- Unprofiled draft+host overhead on HIP is $T_d\approx 3.2\text{ ms}$ (2.11 ms block + 0.71 ms MXFP4 head + 0.20 ms ingest + ~0.2 ms host Markov; note `step0 0.70 ms` includes profile sync). Cross-check: at mean $R\approx 3.3$ and 2.36 tok/pass (measured short greedy, cut 0.5 k3), $2.36 / (19.5 + 3.2)\text{ ms} = 104.0\text{ tok/s}$ (modelled) vs **103.45 tok/s** (measured).
- **If we fine-tune DSpark** (LoRA or full 318M KL-distillation on 5–20M self-distilled greedy tokens + 4 taps, extending `intarsia mtp-dump` [tuning.md](docs/tuning.md#L62-L83)):
  - *Where & cost:* **Requires the 2× GB10 Sparks (separate ask)**—~25–45 GPU-hours total (trajectory+tap dump + BF16/LoRA distillation). Tatooine's ≤5 min windows and 32 GB VRAM cannot train it; endor throttles under sustained load to ~43 W ([2026-11-25-inkling2-mode.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-mode.md#L3-L6), [L158-L159](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-mode.md#L158-L159)).
  - *Modelled gain:* A realistic +3–4 pp general gain lifts greedy tok/pass 2.36→2.52 at $R\approx 3.4$ ($2.52 / 22.9\text{ ms} = 110\text{ tok/s}$, **+6% modelled**); an optimistic domain-matched +7–8 pp lift reaches ~2.75 tok/pass ($2.75 / 23.1\text{ ms} = 119\text{ tok/s}$, **+15% modelled**).

**Cheaper spec-path levers that beat training now:**
1. **HIP `kmax=4..7` + cut sweep (never tested on HIP):** Metal's `cut 0.5 k5` gained ≤1.5% ([2026-11-26-inkling2-spec-cut.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-spec-cut.md#L71-L76)) because Metal's extra verify row costs **+2.9–3.16 ms** ([2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L553), [L737](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L737)) and P12 `in_proj`/`wo` drops back to bf16 at $R\ge 5$ (`DENSE_ROWS_MAX = 4`, [p12.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/quant/p12.rs#L42)). HIP's 4th row costs only **+1.80 ms** ($r\approx 0.14$). In [analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L56-L61), raising `cum prod >= 0.5` from `kmax=3` to `kmax=5..7` increases greedy tokens/pass from 2.50→3.28 (+31%) at mean $R=4.27$ (+1.24 rows ≈ +2.0 ms on HIP → **+12–18% modelled** on high-acceptance greedy/4k/code, neutral on prose).
2. **Sampled DSpark via `engine/keyed.rs`:** Default serving runs `temperature > 0` (`generation_config.json`; [tuning.md](docs/tuning.md#L36)), where DSpark currently skips **100%** of requests ([2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L465-L466), [L1112](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L1112); [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L185-L187)). The softcap verify bug is already fixed and tested on Metal and HIP (`inkling2_verify_softcap`; [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L114-L123), [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L238)). Wiring DSpark into the exact keyed/rejection verify path (which gave +27–30% at `T=1.0` on 35B; [tuning.md](docs/tuning.md#L244)) delivers **+20–35% modelled** to real chat traffic.
3. **Host n-gram bypass before DSpark:** Checking a prompt/output suffix match (length ≥3) on host (<5 µs) before running DSpark skips the 2.1–3.7 ms draft pass on verbatim spans (code edits, RAG, JSON schemas) while staying 100% bitwise.
4. **Verify-pass byte cuts (P12 / FP8 on HIP; `ink-mtp-dn` union fused down on Metal):** On Metal, NVFP4 down at $T=4$ runs at only 318 GB/s (89 µs vs 52 µs floor), leaving 1.0–1.5 ms/pass (~4–6% spec decode, already designed in [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L822-L890)).
5. **Tree / multi-candidate verify — skip:** Adding tree nodes expands the 128-expert top-8 union (+1.5–3 ms/node) and breaks linear K=4 conv rings and rows-exact attention across 48 visits for only ~0.15–0.20 tok/pass.

### 2. Custom IntarsiaML Quant

**Byte budget vs. the 5.49 GB/tok floor** ([2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L241-L254), [2026-11-25-inkling2-decroof.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-decroof.md#L140-L156), [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md#L45-L57)):

| Format / Scope | Eligibility | Calibration | Active GB/tok (Δ vs 5.49) | R9700 floor / plain decode | Metal plain decode (vs 79.0 cur.) |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Native bf16 + experts (HIP today) | Default (HIP) | None | 5.49 (—) | 114 / **77.4** (meas.) | 64.2 pre-P12 (meas.) |
| **P12** (`in_proj`, `wo`, `unembed`) | **Default-eligible (lossless)** | None | 4.62 (-0.88) | 136 / **~86 (+11%)** (mod.) | **79.0** w/ screen (meas.) |
| **FP8 E4M3** (`in_proj` per-seg/row, `wo`, `unembed`) | Opt-in (lossy) | None (RTN) | 3.75 (-1.74) | 167 / **~98 (+27%)** (mod.) | **~88 (+11%)** w/ fast `wo` (mod.) |
| **Mixed NVFP4 + FP8** (NVFP4 bulk+unembed; FP8 `wr_du`, `sink`, `sconv_proj` + sensitive) | Opt-in (lossy) | AWQ/GPTQ + KL search | 3.03 (-2.46) | 207 / **~109 (+41%)** (mod.) | **~98 (+24%)** (mod.) |

*Modelling basis:* Plain step = fixed non-dense/expert latency (~4.2 ms on R9700, ~4.9 ms on Metal; [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md#L50-L57), [2026-11-25-inkling2-decroof.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-decroof.md#L156)) + experts (~4.0 ms) + dense+unembed time scaled by byte reduction at ~93% of bf16 bandwidth.

**Per-tensor decisions:**
- **Lossless P12 on HIP (default-eligible):** Proven bit-exact across all 177 tensors (8.21e-5 escapes, 12.016 bpw, 2.214→1.663 GB unique; [2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L135-L137)), already default-on on Metal (+8.5–9.7%; [L8-L9](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L8-L9)) and CUDA ([p12.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/quant/p12.rs#L351)), unbuilt on HIP ([2026-11-25-inkling2-hip.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-hip.md#L22)). Because prefill only runs 1 unembed row per sequence ([2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md#L193)), HIP can drop the bf16 unembed copy entirely (+0.74 GB net VRAM, fitting 32 GB easily).
- **Opt-in FP8 dense + unembed:** Already built on Metal and passes G3 uncalibrated (short KL21 8.96e-3 CPU / 9.58e-3 Metal vs 1.05e-2; long 1.28e-2 / 1.42e-2 vs 1.65e-2; [2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L193), [L204](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L204)). Two concrete fixes unlock it on both targets: (1) replace the single scale shared across `[q|r|gate|sink]` ([inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L588-L610)) with per-subtensor or per-row scales to eliminate the 1 Metal confident miss; (2) port fused e4m3 `InkInProj` to HIP ([2026-11-25-inkling2-hip-mix.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-hip-mix.md#L48)) and speed up Metal's 295 GB/s `MatmulFp8` `wo` GEMV ([2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L243), [L250](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L250)).
- **Opt-in calibrated mixed NVFP4+FP8 dense + unembed:** Plain RTN MXFP4 failed G3 (KL 2.05–3.75e-2, 2–8 confident misses; [2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L194-L195), [L223-L226](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L223-L226)), specifically due to coarse per-32 power-of-two scales and outlier-sensitive `wr_du`/clipped `q`/`k`. NVFP4 (per-16 e4m3 scales; existing fast kernels on both Metal and HIP, e.g. `nvfp4_gemv_v2_kernel` on 35B's 248k head in [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec3.md#L227)) on `wq_du`/`gate_attn`/`wk_dv`/`wv_dv`/`wo`/`unembed` + FP8 on `wr_du`, `attn_sink_proj`, `sconv.weight_proj`, calibrated via AWQ/GPTQ + a KL-driven sensitivity search upgrading the top-K worst layers to FP8 on a held-out corpus until G3 passes with 0 confident misses, captures 2.46 GB/tok without new exotic kernel families.
- **Experts, controllers, KV cache:** Keep NVFP4 experts (L0–19), MXFP8 coda experts (L20–23: quantizing to NVFP4 saves only 0.12 GB/tok; [2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L252)), and controllers+router (0.15 GB) untouched. Leave KV bf16 for short–4k (KV is only 0.30 GB/tok at 4k; [L56](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L56)); FP8 KV only pays at ≥32k (1.24 GB/tok; [L57](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L57)) or batched VRAM limits.

**Is an "IntarsiaML checkpoint format" worth it?** **Yes—as standard `safetensors` + metadata (not a bespoke binary container), primarily for calibrated quants and Metal zero-copy/cold-start.** Calibrated NVFP4+FP8 *must* live on disk. Beyond that, pre-packing saves real load-time and RAM overhead: today `load()` de-interleaves `w13` into fresh host copies (breaking zero-copy mmap for ~10.4 GB of experts on Metal; [inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L711-L766)), skips 636 MB of vision weights, and spends 4.7–5.9 s folding DSpark's `[200058, 256]` Markov head ([2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L461), [L1110](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L1110)). Pre-baking de-interleaved `w13` + pre-folded DSpark + calibrated weights into `safetensors` + `intarsia_quant.json` (parsed by `QuantScheme`, [scheme.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/quant/scheme.rs#L124-L325)) cuts cold start from ~8 s to <1.5 s and cuts disk/RAM by ~2.5 GB. Keep P12 packing on-the-fly for stock checkpoints (0.7 s; [2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L129-L131)).

### 3. Interaction & Ordering

- **Quantized target → drafter acceptance:** Lossless P12 has **zero** effect on acceptance (proven identical 69.6% / 2.47 tok/pass on Metal; [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L1070-L1072)). Opt-in FP8 dense shifts target top-1 by ~1.5–1.8% ([2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L208)), modelling a ~1–1.5 pp acceptance drop (~-2.5% tok/pass); calibrated NVFP4+FP8 shifts top-1 ~2–3% (~-5% tok/pass). Both are heavily outweighed by the -14% to -22% verify-pass speedup.
- **Quantized target → verify cost & optimal `k`:** Dense weights are read *once* per verify pass, while extra rows grow with MoE expert union (unchanged). Shrinking fixed dense cost raises the marginal-row ratio $r = \Delta T_v / T_v(1)$ (e.g. R9700 $0.15\to 0.21$), so optimal cumulative `cut` shifts slightly up (~0.50→0.55–0.60) and plain decode gains a larger % than spec decode.
- **Quantizing the drafter itself:** The draft head is **already MXFP4 by default** ([dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L409-L424)). `INTARSIA_DSPARK_LAYERS=fp8` already exists ([dspark.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/dspark.rs#L321-L337)) and **regressed Metal** (68.5→64.0 tok/s; [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L503), [L508](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L508)). On HIP the draft block is only 2.11 ms (~6–9% of a cycle; [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-spec.md#L102-L103)), capping any draft-layer quant at ≤1.5%. Keep MXFP4 head + bf16 draft layers (optionally A/B existing `DSPARK_LAYERS=fp8` on HIP once FP8 GEMV is fast).
- **Ordering:** (1) Default-eligible lossless & zero-training spec wins → (2) Opt-in FP8 dense → (3) Opt-in calibrated NVFP4+FP8 pre-packed checkpoint → (4) Only *after* target numerics are frozen, optionally fine-tune DSpark on the quantized target's own taps.

### Don't Do

1. **Train a drafter from scratch (EAGLE-3 / new DFlash / small LM):** Stock DSpark already hits 72–82% conditional greedy acceptance ([analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L15-L16)); beating it from scratch needs 100M+ tokens and hundreds of GPU-hours.
2. **Tree / multi-candidate verify:** Extra tree nodes inflate the 128-expert top-8 union ([analysis.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp/analysis.md#L4-L6)) and break linear K=4 ring convs and rows-exact attention for only ~0.15–0.20 tok/pass.
3. **Uncalibrated MXFP4 dense/unembed:** Already tried and killed—fails G3 with 2–8 confident misses and KL 2.05–3.75e-2 ([2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L194-L195), [L223-L226](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-fp8.md#L223-L226)).
4. **Screened head on verify passes (or 1-row HIP screen before P12):** Verify screen was already tried and killed on Metal (0.991–1.004×; [2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md#L3), [L16-L18](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md#L16-L18)); 1-row screen yields ≤1.4% under default DSpark ([2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-cuda-attn.md#L1995-L1998)).
5. **Quantize the 4 MXFP8 coda expert layers (L20–23) to NVFP4:** Saves only 0.12 GB/tok (2.2%; [2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L252)) while risking output quality.
6. **FP8/4-bit DSpark draft layers on Metal (or custom 4-bit draft layers):** `INTARSIA_DSPARK_LAYERS=fp8` already regressed Metal (68.5→64.0 tok/s; [2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L503), [L508](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-mtp.md#L508)); draft block is only 2.1 ms on HIP.
7. **Bespoke 6-bit or EXL3 dense kernels for Inkling-2:** FP8 passes G3 uncalibrated, NVFP4 kernels already exist on HIP & Metal, and EXL3 adds rotation/helper overhead ([2026-11-02-dsv41-draftdev.md](docs/results/2026-11-02-dsv41-draftdev.md#L245)) with no HIP/Metal Inkling kernels.
8. **FP8 KV cache for short–4k single-stream decode:** Saves <0.15 GB/tok (<2% at 4k; [2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md](docs/plans/2026-11-21-inkling2-port.md#L56)) while requiring ring/attention rewrites across 48 visits ([inkling2.rs](crates/intarsia-core/src/model/inkling2.rs#L393-L411)).
9. **Sub-12-bit lossless entropy coding (ANS/Huffman) or P12 sidecar for stock checkpoints:** bf16 entropy is ~11–11.5 bits (breaks coalesced GEMV for <5% bytes), and load-time P12 packing is already 0.7 s ([2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L129-L131), [L201-L202](docs/results/2026-11-21-inkling2-pack12.md#L201-L202)).
10. **Fused `InkRoute` (op 76) on HIP or Metal `DSPARK_ATTN_ROWS`:** Both already tried and closed as slower/noise (-0.15% HIP [2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec2.md](docs/results/2026-11-26-inkling2-hip-dec2.md#L66-L68); CI 1.000–1.034 Metal [2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md](docs/results/2026-11-25-inkling2-verify.md#L3)).
